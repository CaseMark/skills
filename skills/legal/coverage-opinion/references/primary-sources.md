## How these sources were checked

All checks were made on 2026-10-30 through the case.dev research API. "Full text via case.dev" means the page or opinion named in the URL column was retrieved and the statement was compared with the passage read, not a search snippet. Case citations were also run through the case.dev citation verifier (`/legal/v1/verify`), which matched them to CourtListener records unless noted. "Citation verified only" means the citation matched but the holding stated rests on a later opinion's quotation, as described. Statutes change: before an opinion goes out, re-read the current text of every statute relied on; Ground Rule 4 in SKILL.md requires it.
