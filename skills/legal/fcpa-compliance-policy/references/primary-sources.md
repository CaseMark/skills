## How these sources were checked

All checks were made on 2026-10-30 through the case.dev research API. "Full text via case.dev" means the page or document at the URL was retrieved and the statement compared with the passage read, not a search snippet. Case citations were also run through the case.dev citation verifier (`/legal/v1/verify`), which matched each to a CourtListener record. A mirror is labeled as one. Statutes and enforcement policies change: Ground Rule 4 in SKILL.md requires re-reading the current text before the policy is adopted.
