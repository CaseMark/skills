## How these sources were checked

All checks were made on 2026-10-30 through the case.dev research API. "Full text via case.dev" means the page named in the URL column was retrieved and the statement was compared with the passage read, not a search snippet. Case citations were also run through the case.dev citation verifier, which matched each to a CourtListener record. "As quoted in" means the holding stated rests on a later opinion's description that was read in full. The eCFR is the current unofficial compilation; re-read the current text of every statute and regulation relied on before an opinion goes out (Ground Rule 4 in SKILL.md).
