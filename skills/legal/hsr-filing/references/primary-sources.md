## How these sources were checked

All checks were made on 2026-10-30 through the case.dev API: `/search/v1/contents` to read the full text of each page or PDF, `/search/v1/search` to find sources. "Full text via case.dev" means the whole document or the whole cited section was read; "search excerpt" means only a result summary was seen. eCFR is an unofficial continuously updated edition of the CFR; law.cornell.edu is an unofficial compilation of the U.S. Code. Re-check every row marked "annual" or "status" on the drafting date.
