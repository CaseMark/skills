# Crosby Guaranteed Quality Legal

Quality assurance framework for AI-assisted legal drafting with human attorney verification and malpractice-backed guarantees.

## Prerequisites

- Registered law firm structure with malpractice insurance coverage
- Human attorney on staff for expert judgment and quality sign-off
- Document version control and audit trail capability

## Workflow

1. **AI drafting**: AI agent generates initial draft based on organization's playbook, precedent library, and specific instructions
2. **Quality gate 1**: Automated checks — formatting compliance, clause completeness, conflict detection against organization playbook
3. **Expert judgment**: Designated attorney reviews non-standard items, complex provisions, and flagged risk areas
4. **Quality gate 2**: Final review confirming all agreed terms are present, all objections are addressed, and risk disclosures are accurate
5. **Guaranteed delivery**: Document released with quality guarantee — the law firm stands behind all delivered work product

## Pitfalls

- Quality gates are mandatory — never bypass human review for high-value or complex documents
- The malpractice guarantee only covers documents that passed through all quality gates
- AI agents must be explicitly scoped — quality guarantees do not extend to work outside the assigned agent's configured scope
- Audit trails must be maintained for all quality gate decisions to support insurance claims and regulatory inquiries
