# CTD Module 2 Nonclinical Structure

Heading structure and content rules for the Nonclinical Overview (2.4) and the Nonclinical Written and Tabulated Summaries (2.6), taken from ICH M4S(R2). Status of each rule is recorded in `primary-sources.md`.

## Principles

- The Written and Tabulated Summaries are a comprehensive factual synopsis. Interpretation, clinical relevance, links to quality and implications for safe use belong in the Overview.
- The Overview should not exceed about 30 pages. The three Written Summaries together should in general not exceed 100 to 150 pages. A short IND may be far shorter.
- Citations to the Tabulated Summaries take the form `(Table X.X, Study/Report Number)`.
- The guideline sets a format, not which studies are required. Keep a heading even when no study exists and say so.
- Authors may modify the format where it gives a better presentation.

## Presentation Rules

- In vitro studies precede in vivo studies.
- Order multiple studies by species, then route, then duration (shortest first).
- Species order: mouse, rat, hamster, other rodent, rabbit, dog, non-human primate, other non-rodent mammal, non-mammal.
- Route order: the intended human route first, then oral, intravenous, intramuscular, intraperitoneal, subcutaneous, inhalation, topical, other.
- Use consistent units throughout; a unit conversion table helps.
- Discuss age- and gender-related effects, and findings with stereoisomers and metabolites, where relevant.
- In Discussion and Conclusions, integrate across studies and species, and relate animal exposure to human exposure at the maximum intended dose.

## Module 2.4 Headings

M4S lists six headings in this sequence. They are not numbered in M4S, and the FDA table of contents has a single 2.4 node. Do not present "2.4.1" style numbers as ICH numbering; a sponsor may number internally.

| Heading | Content |
|---|---|
| Overview of the nonclinical testing strategy | Strategy and its justification; GLP status of submitted studies; any deviation from relevant guidelines, justified; association with quality, clinical results and related products; for non-biotech products, assessment of impurities and degradants; for biotech products, comparability of material used in nonclinical and clinical studies; safety of any novel excipient |
| Pharmacology | Evaluation of pharmacodynamic effects, mode of action and potential side effects |
| Pharmacokinetics | Relevance of analytical methods and models; interspecies metabolism and systemic exposure (AUC, Cmax, other parameters) in animals and humans; inconsistencies; anti-product antibodies where relevant |
| Toxicology | Onset, severity, duration, dose dependence, reversibility, species and sex differences; target organs; genotoxicity, carcinogenicity, reproductive and developmental findings, local tolerance, other; exposure at NOAEL and toxic doses against human exposure at the maximum recommended dose, with tables or figures |
| Integrated overview and conclusions | Characteristics of the product shown by the studies; conclusions supporting safety for the intended clinical use; implications for safe human use |
| List of literature references | References cited |

| 2.6.5 | Pharmacokinetics Tabulated Summary | See templates below |

Pivotal studies are the definitive GLP studies M3(R2) specifies; non-pivotal studies may be summarized in less detail.

`*` marks templates the guideline calls optional; text tables in the Written Summary are preferred.

## Where This Skill's Extras Go

- Exposure-multiple table and starting-dose derivation: Module 2.4 Toxicology, with a pointer from 2.6.6.9 Discussion and Conclusions.
- Reviewer checklist, issues list and calculation ledger: outside the CTD text; deliver beside it.
- Page-level source locators: add after the CTD cross-reference citation; they are this skill's traceability layer.

## Biologics Notes

- The impurities and degradants assessment is replaced by a comparability assessment of material used across nonclinical, clinical and proposed marketed supply.
- Species selection is justified by pharmacological relevance (receptor or epitope expression), not by the default rodent and non-rodent pair.
- Add anti-product antibody findings to toxicokinetic and toxicology discussion.
- Record that standard genotoxicity and standard carcinogenicity bioassays were not conducted, with the reason, rather than leaving the headings blank.
