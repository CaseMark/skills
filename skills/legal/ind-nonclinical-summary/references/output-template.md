# Output Template

Layout for the six-part deliverable. Keep the part order. Replace bracketed text; keep every heading even when a part is empty, and say why it is empty.

## Contents

- Part 1: Study inventory
- Part 2: Issues and gaps
- Part 3: Module 2.6
- Part 4: Module 2.4
- Part 5: Calculation ledger
- Part 6: Reviewer checklist

## Part 1: Study Inventory

Opening lines: modality and pathway chosen, population, sponsor inputs received, sponsor inputs missing.

| Study ID | Title | Test article and lot | Test system | Route, regimen, duration | GLP status | Completed | Amendments | CTD section | Pivotal |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Part 2: Issues and Gaps

Rank by severity (Critical, Major, Minor). One row each.

| # | Severity | Issue | Source citation | Who can resolve | Effect on the draft |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Always check for, and list first when present: missing sponsor human-exposure input; conflicts between report text and tables; study director amendments; non-GLP pivotal studies; species with no relevance evidence (proteins); formulation or lot differences across studies; parameters `Not reported`; OCR-uncertain values; modality or population that triggers the oncology overlay.

## Part 3: Module 2.6

Headings in this order, each with the Brief Summary where the structure reference calls for one:

```
2.6.1 Introduction
2.6.2 Pharmacology Written Summary (2.6.2.1 to 2.6.2.7)
2.6.3 Pharmacology Tabulated Summary
2.6.4 Pharmacokinetics Written Summary (2.6.4.1 to 2.6.4.10)
2.6.5 Pharmacokinetics Tabulated Summary
2.6.6 Toxicology Written Summary (2.6.6.1 to 2.6.6.10)
2.6.7 Toxicology Tabulated Summary
```

Each study paragraph: design in one sentence, key findings, NOAEL or equivalent as the report states it, exposure, then citations `(Table X.X, Study ID) [Study ID, Section, Table, p.]`. In 2.6.6.1, include the toxicology programme table: study type and duration, route, species, compound administered, with no results and a GLP comment.

## Part 4: Module 2.4

```
Overview of the nonclinical testing strategy
Pharmacology
Pharmacokinetics
Toxicology      (include the exposure-multiple table)
Integrated overview and conclusions
List of literature references
```

In Toxicology, open with target organs and reversibility, then the exposure multiples at the first and maximum human dose, then the starting-dose rationale for the pathway used. State what the nonclinical package cannot predict for humans.

## Part 6: Reviewer Checklist

```
For the qualified toxicologist or regulatory affairs reviewer. Confirm each item.
[ ] Every NOAEL, STD10 or HNSTD matches the study report and any amendment
[ ] Species choice for the starting dose and the reason
[ ] Safety factor and its justification
[ ] Human exposure and dose projections come from the sponsor and are current
[ ] Exposure basis (parameter, day, analyte, matrix, total or unbound) is the same on both sides of each multiple
[ ] GLP status of each study and the statement or reason the sponsor will give for each covered study
[ ] Pathway fit: modality, population and any oncology overlay
[ ] Every [VERIFY] item and every [SPONSOR INPUT REQUIRED] item resolved
[ ] Gaps listed in Part 2 accepted, filled or justified
[ ] Citations spot-checked against the source reports
```

When a sponsor template or Word document is supplied, preserve its paragraph numbering, heading styles, fonts, headers and footers, and place this content into it; do not rebuild the template.
