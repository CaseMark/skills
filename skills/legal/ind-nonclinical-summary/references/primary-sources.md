## Watch Items

- FDA published a direct final rule titled "Nonclinical Testing Terminology" (91 FR 59988) that replaces "animal" with "nonclinical" in parts of 21 CFR 312 and defines the term. Check its effective status before quoting any regulation text. The skill itself uses "nonclinical" either way.
- The S7B Q&A R1 revision is recent; check which version the sponsor relied on.
