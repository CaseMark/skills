# Expected behavior: Test 04, waived minimum, documented floor and policy floor

## Must

- Section 4 contains an Item 11 analysis for each issuer, laid out as the four questions (stated minimum, waiver authority, non-waivable floor, accepted below-minimum subscriptions with outside-investor status).
- Main Fund: stated minimum $5,000,000 (LPA s. 3.2; PPM p. 7); waiver in the General Partner's sole discretion; a non-waivable floor of $1,000,000 for any person other than a Larkspur Affiliate (LPA s. 3.2 second proviso); accepted below-minimum subscriptions: Covewood $2,500,000 (outside investor, above the floor) and the Whitcombe Family Trust $250,000 (a Larkspur Affiliate and not an outside investor, so excluded). Recommended Item 11 value: $1,000,000. Alternatives stated with the reading behind each (for example $2,500,000 as the lowest accepted outside amount, 0 under the waiver interpretation, $5,000,000 as the stated minimum) and why each is not recommended. Issuer confirmation required: no outside investor below $1,000,000 has been or will be accepted and the proviso has not been amended.
- Parallel Fund: stated minimum $5,000,000 (Parallel LPA s. 3.2; Supplement p. 3); sole discretion with no floor in any governing or offering document; the Subscription Acceptance Policy's $500,000 floor identified as an internal policy, amendable by the Investment Committee, that binds for Item 11 only if the issuer confirms it is the amount below which no waiver will be granted; accepted below-minimum subscription Elm Street Partners $750,000 (outside investor). Recommended value conditional: $500,000 if the issuer confirms the policy floor applies to the Parallel Fund; otherwise 0 under C&DI 130.03. Both alternatives presented with the confirmation named.
- The register's "Minimum investment per PPM: $5,000,000" for both vehicles is recorded as a conflict with the executed subscriptions and is not used.
- Main Item 13(b) $77,750,000; Parallel Item 13(b) $30,750,000; program sold $108,500,000; program remaining $141,500,000; Main Item 15 $250,000 (2 percent of $12,500,000), estimate, payer the Investment Manager.

## Must not

- Report $5,000,000 as the Item 11 value for either issuer.
- Report $250,000 (the affiliate trust's commitment) or $750,000 (the lowest accepted outside amount in the Parallel Fund) as an Item 11 value.
- State categorically that the Parallel Fund's Item 11 is 0 because of waiver discretion without presenting the policy-floor alternative and the confirmation required, or categorically that it is $500,000 without stating that the policy is internal and must be confirmed.
- Treat the Whitcombe Family Trust as an outside investor.
