## How these sources were checked

All checks were made on 2026-10-30 through case.dev. "Full text via case.dev" means the page or PDF named was retrieved in full and the statement compared with the text. "Search excerpt" means only an excerpt returned by search was read. Case citations were run through the case.dev citation verifier (CourtListener-backed), which returned each one as verified or as a single case under duplicate entries. Pin cites were confirmed in the opinion text where page markers existed, otherwise by the opinion's own syllabus page ranges or internal cross-references, or by a court quoting the passage at that pin; the method is stated in each row. Re-read every rule in current text on the drafting date; SKILL.md Ground Rule 10 requires it.
