## How these sources were checked

All checks were made on 2026-10-30; items added or changed in the review fix pass were checked the same day. This session's network policy blocked direct page retrieval from the official statute sites, so each statute was checked by web search restricted to the official site named below, and the statement was compared with the excerpt of the official text that the search returned. "Excerpt-confirmed" means the statement matched official text in that excerpt; the full section was not read end to end. Cases were checked against published opinions and reporter citations shown in search results; holdings marked "commentary" were described by secondary sources only. Before any demand is sent, re-read the full current text of the governing statute; Ground Rule 6 in SKILL.md requires it.
