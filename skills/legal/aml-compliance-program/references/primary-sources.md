## How these sources were checked

All checks were made on 2026-10-30 through the case.dev research API (`/search/v1/contents` for full text, `/search/v1/search` to locate documents). "Full text via case.dev" means the page or PDF at the URL was retrieved and the statement compared with the passage read. eCFR pages are the "current" point-in-time view on the date checked; eCFR is not the official legal edition. The Cornell LII U.S. Code pages are an unofficial compilation. A mirror is labeled as one. Re-read the current eCFR text before a program goes for approval; Ground Rule 4 in SKILL.md requires it.

## Corrections to the prior version of this skill

- CTR exemptions: the prior text required "biennial renewal"; 1020.315(d) requires an annual eligibility review and has no biennial renewal.
- CIP, CDD and beneficial ownership records: the prior text said five years after account closure for all; 1010.230(i)(2) starts verification records at the date made.
- OFAC records: the prior text said at least five years; 501.601 now requires ten.
- SAR deadlines: the prior text gave 30 and 60 days for every institution; MSBs have no 60-day extension.
