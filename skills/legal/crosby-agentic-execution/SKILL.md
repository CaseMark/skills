# Crosby Agentic Execution Legal

Execute contract negotiation workflows through specialized AI agents with lawyer oversight and malpractice-insured quality guarantees.

## Prerequisites

- Incoming contract or legal request via Slack, email, or CLM trigger
- Organization's contract playbooks and negotiation position documents
- Designated human attorney for expert judgment on complex or non-standard matters

## Workflow

1. **Request intake**: Receive contract or legal question through any channel (Slack, email, CLM webhook)
2. **Agent assignment**: Route to custom legal agents built on the organization's specific preferences and playbook
3. **Lawyer review**: Human expert judgment applied to tricky issues — agents flag items requiring attorney sign-off
4. **Iterative refinement**: Agents improve with each review cycle, learning from attorney corrections and feedback
5. **Delivery**: Return executed document with guaranteed quality — all work backed by registered law firm malpractice insurance

## Pitfalls

- Agents must be explicitly configured with negotiation boundaries — never allow AI to agree to terms outside playbook parameters
- Expert judgment is mandatory for non-standard clauses, unusual jurisdictions, or high-value transactions
- Fixed-rate pricing model means scope creep must be managed — document changes require new agent assignment
- AI-generated drafts must always carry a human-attorney review checkpoint before transmission to counterparty
