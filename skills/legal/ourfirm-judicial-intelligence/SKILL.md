# OurFirm Judicial Intelligence

Leverage embedded litigation document intelligence and judicial behavioral data to inform case strategy, simulate judge positioning, and predict likely outcomes.

## Prerequisites

- Access to judicial behavior database (20M+ litigation documents)
- Case-specific discovery materials (pleadings, motions, discovery responses)
- Understanding of relevant jurisdiction and procedural rules

## Workflow

1. **Case intake**: Upload case documents and identify the presiding judge(s) and relevant jurisdiction
2. **Judicial profiling**: Retrieve the judge's behavioral profile — ruling patterns, preferred argument styles, citation habits, and historical case outcomes
3. **Agent simulation**: Deploy specialized AI agents that simulate the judge's likely reasoning and response to proposed arguments
4. **Strategy optimization**: Generate recommended argument framing, citation strategy, and risk assessments based on judicial profile data
5. **Document drafting**: Produce litigation documents (motions, briefs, memoranda) optimized for the identified judge's preferences

## Pitfalls

- Judicial intelligence data is probabilistic — it informs strategy but does not replace attorney judgment
- Profiles must be current — judicial behavioral patterns can shift with new precedents and court composition changes
- Never present judicial profiling data as factual predictions in filings — it is strategic background intelligence
- All simulated outputs must be verified against primary legal authority before use in court documents
