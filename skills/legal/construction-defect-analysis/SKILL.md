# Construction Defect Analysis

Document and analyze construction defects to support claims, change orders, and dispute resolution.

## Prerequisites
- Photos or site inspection notes
- Original construction documents and specifications
- Warranty or guarantee terms
- Timeline of defect discovery

## Workflow
1. **Classify**: Categorize defects by type (structural, envelope, MEP, finishes, code)
2. **Document**: Create a defect log with location, photos, description, and severity
3. **Trace**: Link each defect to the responsible trade, phase, or specification gap
4. **Cost Estimate**: Provide rough-order magnitude repair cost estimates
5. **Report**: Generate a formal defect report for claims or litigation support

## Pitfalls
- Distinguish between latent and patent defects — statute of limitations differs
- Don't attribute defects to design without engineering analysis
- Preserve chain of custody for photographic evidence
- Check warranty periods — some defects may be covered, others are not
