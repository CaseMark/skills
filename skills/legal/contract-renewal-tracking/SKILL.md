# Contract Renewal and Obligation Tracking

## Prerequisites
- Portfolio of executed contracts
- Known renewal preferences and negotiation timelines
- Internal approval workflows for renewals

## Workflow
1. **Ingest contracts** — load contract portfolio for analysis
2. **Extract dates and deadlines** — identify renewal dates, auto-renewal triggers, notice periods, milestone dates
3. **Build Events Dashboard** — create centralized view of all contractual obligations and deadlines
4. **Set proactive alerts** — configure notifications for upcoming renewals, notice windows, and payment due dates
5. **Monitor compliance** — track whether obligations are being fulfilled on both sides
6. **Renewal preparation** — surface terms, pricing history, and negotiation options before renewal windows

## Pitfalls
- Auto-renewal clauses can be buried in fine print — verify language carefully
- Notice periods vary widely; do not assume standard timeframes
- Track both sides' obligations — counterparties may also miss their commitments
- Maintain audit trail of all alerts and actions taken
