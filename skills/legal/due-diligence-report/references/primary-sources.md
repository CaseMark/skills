## How these sources were checked

All checks were made on 2026-10-30. Statute, regulation and opinion pages were read in full through the case.dev API (`/search/v1/contents`), and reporter citations were checked with `/legal/v1/verify`. "Full text via case.dev" means the whole section or opinion page was retrieved and the cited passage was read; "described in" means the authority was seen only as quoted or summarized in another source that was read in full. Cornell LII, Justia and OpenJurist are unofficial compilations; the UCC rows read at Cornell are the uniform official text, and state enactments vary unless a state code is listed. Re-check any authority before the report is finalized.
