## Exhibit 2. Selections and method

- Tail factor 60 months to ultimate: 1.020, derived in the study from Schedule P prior-row development.
- Accident years 2021 to 2023: reported development method.
- Accident years 2024 and 2025: Bornhuetter-Ferguson on reported losses with an expected loss ratio of 62.0%, described in the study as "the 2026 board-approved business plan loss ratio".
- Carried IBNR (including DCC) at December 31, 2025, all accident years: 44,775.
