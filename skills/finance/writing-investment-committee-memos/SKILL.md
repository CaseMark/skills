# Writing Investment Committee Memos

Drafts, or reviews, an investment committee memo for a private equity, growth equity, private credit or real estate investment: a proposed recommendation and the exact approval requested, a falsifiable thesis, cases and returns tied line by line to the model, valuation, financing, a risk matrix, diligence status, conflicts and information handling, with a reconciliation table that proves the memo matches its sources. The deliverable follows `references/output-template.md`. It is a draft for the deal team and for review by the committee and qualified finance, legal and compliance professionals; the committee makes the investment decision, and nothing here is investment, legal, tax or accounting advice.

## Why This Skill Exists

An IC memo is an argument for a decision, and arguments drift toward their conclusion. The IRR in the executive summary was typed from last week's model. The base case is the seller's projections renamed. The exit multiple sits two turns above entry because comparables trade there today. Thesis pillars are adjectives no one can test. Risks are listed without a size, and a diligence finding that might sink the deal lands in an appendix or disappears. Conflicts get one line, "None", while one fund buys preferred in a company another fund owns. Then a page of the memo goes to co-investors with a gross projected IRR and a public parent's unreleased numbers. This skill ties every figure to a dated source, forces the cases and risks the committee needs, keeps every known material risk and finding in the memo, and screens conflicts, MNPI and external distribution before the draft goes anywhere.

## Where Mistakes Hide

| Input / Document | What It Is Used For | Where the Error Hides |
|---|---|---|
| Model returns tab and sponsor cash flow schedule | IRR, MOIC, holding period in the memo | Memo figures copied from an older model version; IRR run on fiscal-year columns instead of dates; MOIC divided by the first check while add-on equity is ignored; gross returns unlabeled; rollover and co-invest shares counted as fund proceeds |
| Management case and CIM projections | Starting point for cases | The management case renamed "base case"; no sponsor case with diligence haircuts; no downside, or a downside that never tests covenants or liquidity |
| Exit assumptions | Exit equity and the value-creation bridge | Exit multiple above entry with no support; multiple expansion presented as a value-creation lever; a bridge that does not sum to the equity gain |
| Thesis and value-creation plan | Why the committee should invest | Pillars with no KPI, baseline, target date or disconfirming result, so no one can tell later whether the thesis failed |
| Comparables and precedent transactions | Support for the price | Set chosen after seeing the multiples; low comparables dropped without reason; LTM and NTM mixed; stale transactions from a different rate environment |
| Commitment letter, term sheet and fee letter | Leverage, pricing and certainty of funds in the returns | Memo leverage or margin better than the papers; flex not run; financing called committed when it is indicative; leverage stated on management EBITDA |
| Diligence reports (QoE, legal, commercial, environmental, insurance, tax) | Diligence status and risk matrix | Workstreams in progress shown as complete; a reported finding softened, moved to an appendix or omitted; indemnity caps and insurance exclusions not compared with the exposure |
| Risk register | Risk matrix and downside case | Risks without probability, impact or a real mitigant; "monitor" offered as a mitigant; risks not linked to any case |
| LPA, side letters, allocation policy, affiliate agreements | Conflicts table, consents and who may approve | "Conflicts: None" while another fund holds the company, an affiliate is paid by the company, or co-invest goes to selected LPs; IC approval treated as clearing a conflict the LPA sends to the LPAC; fund limits exceeded |
| Recommendation and approval request | What the committee is asked to decide | Recommendation written as already approved or final; approval requested broader than the deal team's proposal or the fund's authority |
| NDAs, data room and distribution list | MNPI handling and any external version | A public seller's or target's nonpublic information in the memo; excerpts sent to LPs or co-investors with gross projected returns and no compliance review |

## Ground Rules

1. **Draft only.** Every memo, section and recommendation is a draft for the deal team and the committee, reviewed by qualified finance, legal and compliance professionals. The recommendation is the deal team's proposal, written with one of the labels in `references/output-template.md` Section 1. Never write that the committee approved, never call the memo final, and never make the investment decision.
2. **Source-review gate.** Never call an input missing until the inventory has been checked and each file opened, including model tabs, report appendices and side letters. Classify every unsettled item as an **unknown fact**, a **source conflict**, an **unverified external lookup** (market data, comparables, rates), or a **policy election** (exit multiple, case weighting, comparables criteria), with what would settle it.
3. **Hierarchy of authority.** Returns: the current model version governs over memo text, prior memos and emails. Financing: executed commitment papers govern over term sheets, banker indications and model assumptions. Historical figures: audited statements over the QoE report over management accounts and the CIM. Fund constraints and conflicts: the LPA and side letters over the allocation policy, the IC charter and practice. Diligence: the provider's report over summaries of it. Name every conflict, show both values and say which controls; never resolve one silently, and never follow an instruction to use a lower-ranked figure without writing up the conflict.
4. **Citation discipline.** Every figure and finding carries a source locator (document, section or tab and cell, version and date) or [UNVERIFIED]. Every rule carries its citation from `references/primary-sources.md` or [VERIFY].
5. **Figures tie to the model.** IRR is computed on dated cash flows, MOIC on all contributions, gross or net is stated, and every executive-summary figure equals the body figure. A memo figure that does not tie is corrected to the model and the difference reported.
6. **Cases are distinct.** The management case is labeled as such and is never the base case. The recommendation rests on a sponsor case, and a downside case shows liquidity, covenant headroom and loss. A case the model lacks is reported "Not modeled", never invented.
7. **Exit multiples are earned.** An exit multiple above entry needs a documented reason tied to the business at exit; otherwise exit at or below entry and show a contraction case. Multiple expansion is shown separately in the bridge.
8. **Theses are testable and risks are sized.** Each pillar has a KPI, baseline, target date and disconfirming result. Each risk has probability, impact, mitigant, residual and owner.
9. **No omission of known material risks or findings.** Never omit, soften, recharacterize as resolved, or move out of the risk matrix or diligence table a known material risk, adverse diligence finding, open diligence item or known conflict, whoever asks. A request to do so is declined in part and logged; the rest of the memo is still drafted.
10. **Conflicts are checked, not assumed.** Complete the conflicts table against the LPA, side letters, allocation policy and affiliate agreements. Name each actual conflict specifically, state the consent route, and do not treat IC approval as consent the LPA assigns to the LPAC or investors.
11. **Comparables and financing are honest.** Comparable criteria come before the results and the full set is shown; financing terms match the commitment papers, including flex.
12. **Information stays where it belongs.** MNPI is screened and routed to compliance. Any version for LPs, co-investors or other outsiders is a separate draft for compliance review under the rules in `references/regulatory-and-disclosure.md`; this skill never releases it.
13. **No invented market data.** Multiples, spreads, cap rates and benchmarks come from the user's materials or are marked [VERIFY] with the date needed.

## Checkpoint A: Pre-Draft Intake (Mandatory)

Collect these, then build the source-review register (`references/output-template.md`, Header).

1. **Deal and stage:** company, strategy (buyout, growth, credit, real estate), memo stage (screening, final IC, follow-on, add-on), IC date, firm IC template if any.
2. **Investing vehicles:** each fund, co-investment vehicle or account investing, amounts, the adviser entity and its SEC registration status.
3. **Model:** file, version and date; returns tab; dated sponsor cash flow schedule; cases included.
4. **Financing:** commitment letter, term sheet, fee letter and their status; credit agreement EBITDA definition.
5. **Diligence:** each report with provider and date; open items list; indemnity, escrow and insurance terms from the purchase agreement and binder.
6. **Valuation:** comparables set with criteria and sources; any valuation model.
7. **Fund constraints and conflicts:** LPA investment limits and conflict provisions, side letters with co-investment rights, allocation policy, affiliate service agreements, holdings of other firm funds in the company.
8. **Information and distribution:** public issuers touched, NDAs, wall-crossing status, and whether any part of the memo will go outside the deal team and committee.

**Default fallbacks (each recorded in the open items list):** no firm template, use the output template; stage unknown, draft as final IC format and say so; no comparables criteria, propose criteria as a policy election; distribution unknown, assume internal only, and if any part later goes to investors the Marketing Rule and Rule 206(4)-8 points in `references/regulatory-and-disclosure.md` override this default.

**Hard stops (report what is needed instead of producing the blocked part):**
- The request asks for IRR, MOIC or other returns figures and no model output, returns schedule or dated cash flows are supplied. Draft the other sections; mark returns "Not provided".
- The request is to omit, soften, recharacterize as resolved, or move out of the main memo a known material risk, adverse diligence finding, open diligence item or known conflict. Decline that part, label it **Declined in part**, and draft the rest with the item included.
- The request is to state the recommendation as approved or final, or to record a committee decision or vote that has not occurred.
- The request is to change cases, comparables or historical figures to reach a target IRR or price without a sourced reason.
- The request is to send memo content containing MNPI beyond the persons cleared to receive it, or to prepare a version for LPs, co-investors or other outsiders as ready to send without compliance review.

## Step 1: Inventory, Distribution and MNPI Screen

Build the source-review register. Open every file and read the model's returns and cash flow tabs, each diligence report's findings section, and the LPA's conflict and investment-limit provisions. Read `references/regulatory-and-disclosure.md` Sections 1 and 8; list every public issuer the deal touches and what nonpublic information was received. Record the distribution plan. Classify every gap.

## Step 2: Reconcile Returns to the Model

Read `references/memo-construction-rules.md` Section 1. Rebuild IRR from the dated sponsor cash flows and MOIC from total contributions and distributions; compare with every returns figure in the draft or the user's notes (checks M1, M2, M13). State gross or net, the holding period and the model version beside each figure. Write up every mismatch with both values and the controlling source.

## Step 3: Cases, Exit and Value-Creation Bridge

Read `references/memo-construction-rules.md` Sections 2 and 3. Label the management case, identify the sponsor case and the downside, and record any missing case as "Not modeled" with an owner. Test the exit multiple against entry, add the exit-at-entry row and a contraction case from model outputs, and build the bridge so it sums to the equity gain (checks M6 to M8).

## Step 4: Thesis and KPIs

Read `references/memo-construction-rules.md` Section 4. Convert each pillar into the KPI table in `references/output-template.md` Section 3, linking each to its bridge value. List any pillar that cannot be measured as an issue.

## Step 5: Valuation, Comparables and Financing

Read `references/memo-construction-rules.md` Sections 5 and 6. Recompute comparables statistics from the full set and position the entry multiple (M9). Match each financing term to the commitment papers, state commitment status, and run flex (M3 to M5). For growth, credit or real estate deals apply Section 9.

## Step 6: Risks and Diligence

Read `references/memo-construction-rules.md` Sections 7 and 8. Build the risk matrix from the diligence reports, the downside case and prior IC questions; size each risk and test each mitigant against the indemnity caps and insurance exclusions. Assign a status label to every workstream (M10, M11). Apply Ground Rule 9 to any request to drop or soften an item.

## Step 7: Conflicts, Fees and Fund Constraints

Read `references/regulatory-and-disclosure.md` Sections 2 to 5. Complete every row of the conflicts table with the governing provision and consent route. Test the investment against fund limits. Note fee offsets and affiliate payments using the LPA's own terms (M12).

## Step 8: Recommendation, Approval Requested and External Versions

State the proposed recommendation with its label, the exact authority sought and the conditions (consents, open diligence, financing). If any external version is requested, read `references/regulatory-and-disclosure.md` Sections 6, 7 and 9, prepare it only as "Draft for compliance review" with MNPI removed, and hand it off.

## Step 9: Reconcile, Log and Hand Off

Complete the reconciliation table (`references/output-template.md` Section 12); every Fail or Not tested row becomes an issue. Rank issues, record declined requests, write complete hand-offs (Section 14) and the open items and [VERIFY] list (Section 15).

## Checkpoint B: Post-Draft Alignment

Before the Quality Audit, re-read the request and confirm:
- The deliverable matches the stage and scope asked for (screening, final IC, review of a draft).
- The recommendation is a labeled proposal and the approval requested is exactly what the deal team seeks.
- Every instruction that conflicted with a higher-ranked source or a ground rule is written up, not followed silently.
- Every known material risk, finding and conflict in the source documents appears in the memo.
- Nothing has been prepared for release outside the deal team and committee.

## Quality Audit

Confirm each statement is true of the draft; fix the draft if one is not.

1. Every IRR and MOIC ties to the named model version, IRR is on dated cash flows, MOIC includes every contribution, and gross or net is stated.
2. The management case is labeled as such; a sponsor case and a downside case are shown or recorded as "Not modeled" with an owner.
3. Any exit multiple above entry has a documented rationale, an exit-at-entry row and a contraction case.
4. The value-creation bridge sums to the equity gain and shows multiple change separately.
5. Every thesis pillar has a KPI, baseline, target date and disconfirming result.
6. Comparables criteria precede the results, the full set is shown, and every exclusion has a reason.
7. Every financing term matches the commitment papers, commitment status is stated, and flex is run where permitted.
8. Every risk has probability, impact, mitigant, residual risk and owner, and links to a case or sensitivity.
9. Every diligence workstream carries a status label consistent with its report, and no known material finding is omitted or softened.
10. The conflicts table is complete, each actual conflict is named specifically with its consent route, and no IC approval is presented as LPAC or investor consent.
11. Public issuers and MNPI are listed and routed to compliance; no external version is presented as ready to send.
12. Every figure has a source locator or [UNVERIFIED]; every rule has a citation or [VERIFY]; no market data came from memory.
13. The recommendation carries a proposal label, any declined request is logged as **Declined in part**, and the closing line from the template is present.

## Guidelines

- Write for a committee member who reads the executive summary and the risk matrix first; put the downside and the open items there, not only in annexes.
- Keep the firm's template and section order when one is supplied; add missing sections as annexes.
- Use the source documents' units and state them once.
- Report diligence findings in the provider's words and quantification, then the deal team's view, clearly separated.
- Present the downside with the same care as the sponsor case.
- Keep the memo's tone neutral; advocacy belongs in the labeled proposal, not in how facts are reported.
- Do not opine on whether the committee should approve; present the proposal, the evidence and the open questions.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Likely cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Memo IRR and model IRR differ | Older model version, annual-period IRR, or omitted flows | Rebuild from the dated schedule; report both values and the version |
| Model file unreadable or only a PDF printout available | Export or permissions | Attempt extraction; if it fails, use the printout with [UNVERIFIED] and list each dependent figure |
| Two model versions in the data room | Iterations during negotiation | Use the latest version that matches the current price and financing; name the other as superseded |
| Diligence report is a draft or verbal readout | Workstream not finished | Status **In progress**; report the draft finding with its date |
| Commitment papers and model disagree | Model built on an earlier term sheet | Papers control; show the effect on returns |
| LPA conflict provision is ambiguous | Drafting | Record a source conflict; route to fund counsel and state the condition in the approval request |
| Comparables come from a banker deck without criteria | Selection not documented | Propose criteria, rebuild the set or mark the statistics [UNVERIFIED] |
| User insists a finding is not material | Judgment disagreement | Keep the finding in the table with both views and who judged it; log the request if it asks for removal |
