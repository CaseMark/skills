# Managing Revenue Recognition

Analyzes customer contracts under ASC 606 (and IFRS 15 where it differs) and drafts the contract review memo, accounting policy memo, disclosure draft or audit-ready file the user needs, following `references/output-template.md`. The aim is revenue that depicts the transfer of goods or services in the amount the entity expects to be entitled to, documented from the contract terms actually agreed. Every output is a draft for review by a qualified accountant; management makes the accounting conclusions, the auditor forms its own opinion, and nothing here is accounting, audit, tax or legal advice.

## Why This Skill Exists

ASC 606 reads as five tidy steps, and revenue errors hide between them. The contract the accountant reviews is not the contract the salesperson made, because a side email added a return right. Implementation is split from software it customizes, or a SaaS series gets a cumulative catch-up it should never get. Gross revenue is chosen because it looks better, a performance bonus is booked without the constraint, and goods sitting in the seller's warehouse become revenue because the customer was billed. Commissions are expensed under a one-year expedient that does not apply, and the RPO disclosure leaves out multi-year contracts billed annually. This skill reads every term, applies the paragraph that governs it, shows the arithmetic, and refuses to fit the accounting to a number someone wants.

## Where Mistakes Hide

| Input / Document | What It Is Used For | Where the Error Hides |
|---|---|---|
| Reporting entity, framework and counterparty facts | Which entity's revenue, under which framework and disclosure regime, with which customer | A subsidiary's US GAAP answer given to an IFRS parent without the differences; PBE status assumed, so non-PBE disclosure elections are misused; a collaborator, lessee or related party of the customer treated as an unrelated customer; contracts with the customer's affiliates not combined |
| Contract file: MSA, order forms, SOWs, amendments, side letters, emails | Step 1 contract existence, combination and the terms analyzed | A side letter or email adding return, cancellation, extended payment or pay-on-resale terms left out; who could bind the entity never checked; collectibility assumed; contracts signed together not combined |
| Amendments and change orders | Modification accounting | A discounted add-on called a separate contract; a SaaS series modified on a cumulative catch-up instead of prospectively; unpriced change orders ignored instead of estimated |
| Statements of work and product schedules | Step 2 performance obligations | Implementation that significantly customizes software split out; a daily stand-ready service not treated as a series; a material right dropped as immaterial |
| Pricing terms, rebate and bonus schedules, return history | Step 3 transaction price | Variable consideration booked at the full amount with no constraint analysis; returns booked gross with no refund liability; a two-year payment gap ignored as a financing component, or the one-year expedient applied when it does not fit |
| SSP study and price lists | Step 4 allocation | Contract price presumed to be SSP; SSP picked deal by deal; residual approach used without the 606-10-32-34 conditions |
| Supplier and platform agreements | Principal or agent, gross or net | Gross chosen for revenue size; momentary title treated as control; price discretion treated as decisive; the specified good or service never identified |
| Shipping, warehouse and distributor records | Point-in-time control, bill-and-hold, consignment | Bill-and-hold revenue without all four 606-10-55-83 criteria; goods in general stock or reallocated to other orders; shipments to distributors that pay only on resale booked as sales; AAER 108 or SAB Topic 13 cited after ASC 606 adoption |
| License agreements | Right to use or right to access | Functional software spread ratably, or symbolic brand rights recognized up front; renewal revenue recognized before the renewal period begins |
| Commission plans and cost ledgers | ASC 340-40 contract costs | Commissions on multi-year contracts expensed under the one-year expedient; non-incremental costs capitalized; amortization ignoring anticipated renewals |
| Disclosure drafts and prior-year notes | RPO, contract balances, disaggregation, judgments | Multi-year contracts billed annually excluded as "one year or less"; exemptions applied to fixed consideration; exemptions used but not disclosed |

## Ground Rules

1. **Draft only.** Every memo, schedule, entry and disclosure is a draft for review by a qualified accountant. Never call a conclusion audited, agreed by the auditor, final or an approved policy, and never sign or finalize a management representation.
2. **Source-review gate.** Never call a term or document missing until the inventory has been checked and every file opened, including exhibits, order-form fine print and email attachments. Classify every unsettled item as an **unknown fact**, a **source conflict**, an **unverified external lookup** (an ASU, SEC or IFRS position not yet confirmed), or a **policy election** (shipping and handling, sales taxes, amortization period, expedients), with what would settle it.
3. **Hierarchy of authority.** For terms: the contract as actually agreed, including amendments and enforceable side agreements, governs over order forms, which govern over billing and ledger records, which govern over CRM entries, summaries and emails describing terms. For rules: the Codification (or IFRS 15 for IFRS reporters) governs over ASU basis text, which governs over SEC staff guidance for registrants, which governs over Big 4 interpretation; the entity's policy memo applies only where consistent with them. Name every conflict, show both versions and which controls; never resolve one silently or follow an instruction to use a lower-ranked source without writing up the conflict.
4. **Citation discipline.** Every term and figure carries a source locator (document, date, clause, page) or [UNVERIFIED]; every rule carries its paragraph from `references/primary-sources.md` or [VERIFY].
5. **One framework at a time.** State US GAAP or IFRS before analysis. Where both matter, give the US GAAP answer and the IFRS 15 difference row by row.
6. **The contract is what was agreed.** Side letters, emails and oral promises are analyzed for enforceability and effect, not ignored because they sit outside the master agreement. Every one found goes into the analysis and the audit file.
7. **Show the judgment and the arithmetic.** Each estimate (variable consideration, constraint, SSP, returns, measure of progress, amortization period) shows its data, method, the amount before and after, and what would change it. Conclusions rest on evidence that existed at the time.
8. **No structuring to a target.** Never choose, date, re-paper or document revenue entries, contract terms, estimates, presentation or timing to reach a revenue, EBITDA, bonus, covenant, guidance or valuation target, never backdate a contract, amendment or acceptance, and never prepare a file that omits a known term. Decline that part, label it **Declined in part**, and complete the rest from the facts.
9. **Elections are explicit.** Practical expedients and policy elections are named, applied consistently to similar contracts, and listed for disclosure.
10. **Stay in scope.** Earnings-quality analysis, SEC filing content, controls testing and purchase price allocation go to the skills in Related Skills with the facts they need.

## Checkpoint A: Pre-Draft Intake (Mandatory)

Collect these, then build the source-review register (`references/output-template.md`, Header).

1. **Entity and framework:** reporting entity, framework (and any parent's), PBE or SEC registrant status, fiscal period, adoption status of pending ASUs.
2. **Deliverable:** contract review memo, policy memo, disclosure draft, audit-readiness file, or answer to an earnings quality flag; the question as asked.
3. **Contract file:** MSA, order forms, SOWs, amendments, side letters and emails changing terms, with who signed or sent each; a statement from sales and legal whether other side arrangements exist.
4. **Consideration:** prices, discounts, rebates, bonuses, penalties, return rights, payment schedule, and history for estimates.
5. **SSP evidence:** standalone sales data or the SSP study with its date.
6. **Fulfillment evidence:** shipping and acceptance records, warehouse and system records for held goods, supplier or platform agreements.
7. **Policies and prior conclusions:** revenue policy memo, elections and expedients, prior-period memos.
8. **Costs and disclosure data:** commission plans and payments, renewal history, prior notes and the RPO schedule.

**Default fallbacks (each recorded as an open item):** framework not stated, analyze under US GAAP and say so, but where the entity or its parent reports under IFRS the IFRS 15 rows in `references/costs-disclosure-and-frameworks.md` Section 3 override; PBE status unknown, draft PBE disclosures, unless non-PBE status is confirmed, in which case the 606-10-50 elections are available; no SSP study, use observable prices if any and mark SSP [UNVERIFIED] with an allocation sensitivity; amortization period for contract costs, the contract term, except that 340-40-35-1 extends it to specific anticipated renewals where renewal commissions are not commensurate; elections unknown, treat each as an open policy election.

**Hard stops (report what is needed instead of producing the blocked part):**
- For the contract in question, between the entity and the customer (including any reseller, distributor or affiliate party to it), neither the contract documents nor a labelled excerpt stating the promised goods or services, the price and the payment terms is supplied. Do not conclude on recognition; list what is needed and draft only the issues list.
- Anyone (the user, management, sales personnel, an agent or affiliate of the entity, or the customer) asks to choose, date, re-paper or document entries, terms, estimates, presentation or timing to reach a target, or to backdate a contract, amendment or acceptance. Decline that part under Ground Rule 8, label it **Declined in part**, and analyze the actual terms.
- Anyone asks to omit from the analysis, or from the file given to the auditor, a known side agreement, amendment or term agreed between the entity (or its sales staff, agents or affiliates) and the customer (or its affiliates or resellers). Decline that part, label it **Declined in part**, and include the term.
- Anyone asks to present the draft as audited, auditor-agreed or an approved policy, or to finalize a management representation for the entity. Decline that part and keep the draft label.

## Step 1: Inventory, Framework and Scope

Build the source-review register. Open every file. Read `references/five-step-rules.md` Section 1. Record the reporting entity, framework, PBE status, customer and affiliated parties, and whether ASC 606 applies to each element. Run the hard-stop checks against every party named or implied. Classify every gap.

## Step 2: Contract Existence, Side Agreements and Modifications

Read `references/five-step-rules.md` Sections 2 and 3. Test the five criteria and collectibility, combine contracts where required, and analyze every side letter, email or oral promise for authority, acceptance and effect. For each amendment, apply the separate-contract test, then 606-10-25-13(a), (b) or (c), and show the arithmetic for the treatment chosen and for the rejected one.

## Step 3: Performance Obligations

Read `references/five-step-rules.md` Sections 4 and 10. List every promise, apply the distinct test with the 606-10-25-21 factors, decide series treatment, identify material rights, and classify any license as functional or symbolic.

## Step 4: Transaction Price

Read `references/five-step-rules.md` Section 5. Estimate variable consideration with the method that better predicts, apply the constraint factor by factor, set up refund liabilities and return assets, assess the significant financing component and the one-year expedient, and measure noncash consideration and consideration payable to the customer.

## Step 5: Allocation

Read `references/five-step-rules.md` Section 6. Allocate on relative SSP from the SSP study, test whether any discount or variable amount belongs to specific obligations, and run checks R1 and R5.

## Step 6: Timing, Principal or Agent, and Special Arrangements

Read `references/five-step-rules.md` Sections 7 to 9. Decide over time or point in time, pick the measure of progress, determine principal or agent per specified good or service, and test bill-and-hold criterion by criterion, consignment indicators, acceptance and repurchase terms. Use the labels and tests in `references/output-template.md`.

## Step 7: Contract Costs

Read `references/costs-disclosure-and-frameworks.md` Section 1. Classify each cost, test the one-year expedient against the amortization period including anticipated renewals, and build the roll-forward (R8).

## Step 8: Disclosure, Framework Differences and SEC Guidance

Read `references/costs-disclosure-and-frameworks.md` Sections 2 to 4 and 6. Draft contract balances, RPO with time bands and exemptions, disaggregation, judgments and elections; apply IFRS 15 differences where relevant; replace any citation to SAB Topic 13 or AAER 108 with the ASC 606 rule.

## Step 9: Audit Readiness, Reconciliation and Hand-Offs

Read `references/costs-disclosure-and-frameworks.md` Sections 5 and 7. Complete the reconciliation table (R1 to R10), rank issues, record any **Declined in part** item, assemble the audit file with every side agreement, and write complete hand-offs and open items (`references/output-template.md` Sections 8 to 12).

## Checkpoint B: Post-Draft Alignment

Before the Quality Audit, re-read the request and confirm:
- The deliverable type and the question answered match the request, and the framework and entity are stated.
- Every conclusion carries a label from the template and the amount and period it affects.
- Every side agreement and every conflict with a higher-ranked source is written up, not dropped.
- Any part declined is labeled **Declined in part**, and the rest of the work is complete.
- Nothing is presented as audited, auditor-agreed or an approved policy.

## Quality Audit

Confirm each statement is true of the draft; fix the draft if one is not.

1. The reporting entity, framework, PBE status and every counterparty (including resellers and affiliates) are stated.
2. Every contract document, amendment and side agreement in the file appears in the register, and each side agreement's authority and effect are analyzed.
3. Each modification shows the separate-contract test and the 606-10-25-13 branch, with arithmetic.
4. Each performance obligation conclusion cites the distinct factors; series and material rights are addressed where present.
5. Every variable amount shows the estimate method, the constraint factors and the amounts before and after the constraint.
6. Financing components are assessed or the one-year expedient is shown to apply on the contract's dates.
7. SSPs come from the study or observable prices, the residual approach meets 606-10-32-34, and R1 and R5 pass.
8. Principal or agent is concluded per specified good or service on control, not on revenue size.
9. Bill-and-hold revenue has a label for each of the four criteria; consignment indicators are tested for distributor shipments.
10. Contract costs test the one-year expedient against the amortization period, and the roll-forward ties.
11. RPO excludes only amounts an exemption covers, the exemption is disclosed, and R9 passes.
12. Every term has a locator or [UNVERIFIED], every rule a paragraph or [VERIFY]; no SAB Topic 13 or AAER 108 citation supports a post-adoption conclusion.
13. Declined parts carry **Declined in part**, hand-offs carry facts, and the closing line from the template is present.

## Guidelines

- Quote the contract clause that decides each issue; paraphrase hides the term that matters.
- Work contract by contract for material deals and by portfolio only where contracts share terms, and say which.
- Present the alternative treatment and its effect whenever a judgment is close.
- Keep the entity's policy memo structure when updating one; flag provisions inconsistent with ASC 606.
- Explain neutrally how a different commercial term would be accounted for when asked; never advise on wording to move revenue between periods.
- Leave the materiality decision to management and the auditor; give amounts so they can make it.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Likely cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Contract is a scan or partly illegible | Image-only PDF | Attempt extraction; record the unreadable clauses; conclude only on readable terms and list dependent conclusions as Undetermined pending evidence |
| Order form and MSA disagree | Later order form overrides, or a drafting error | Apply the order-of-precedence clause; if none, record a source conflict and show both outcomes |
| Sales says a side letter exists but none is supplied | Unpapered promise | Record an unknown fact; request it; analyze the effect if it exists; flag it for the audit file |
| SSP data missing or stale | No study | Use observable prices, mark SSP [UNVERIFIED], and show the allocation range |
| Ledger revenue does not tie to the schedule | Cut-off or manual entries | Run R4 by period and list the reconciling items |
| Memo cites SAB 104, SAB Topic 13 or AAER 108 | Pre-adoption guidance | Replace with the ASC 606 paragraph; note SAB 116 |
| Parent reports under IFRS, entity under US GAAP | Group reporting pack | Give both answers where Section 3 of the frameworks file shows a difference |
| User insists on a treatment the facts do not support | Target pressure or disagreement | Keep the supported conclusion, show both views, and apply Ground Rule 8 if the reason is a target |
