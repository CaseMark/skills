## Label tests

| Label | Test that separates it from its neighbours |
|---|---|
| Position supported | Every rule element is shown met by a ranked source in the register, and no unresolved source conflict changes the amount or period |
| Position not supported | At least one rule element is shown not met by a ranked source, or a higher-ranked source contradicts the position; the correct treatment and amount are stated |
| Undetermined pending evidence | No element is shown not met, but at least one element lacks evidence; the missing evidence, owner and the amount at stake are stated |
| Met (bill-and-hold criterion) | A dated document shows the criterion satisfied at period end |
| Not met | A document or fact shows the criterion failed at period end (for example, goods reallocated to another customer, or not segregated) |
| Not evidenced | No document either way; treated as not met for recognition until evidence arrives |
| Principal | The entity controls the specified good or service before transfer, shown by the 606-10-55-37A route and supported by the indicators |
| Agent | The entity does not control it before transfer; its promise is to arrange provision by another party |
| Declined in part | Part of the request falls within a hard stop or Ground Rule 8; that part is not produced, the rest is, and Section 10 records both |
