# Managing Debt Covenant Compliance

Prepares the periodic covenant compliance package for a borrower group, or reviews one for an agent or lender: a covenant term sheet, a Consolidated EBITDA bridge under the agreement's own definition, ratio calculations with a status from a fixed vocabulary, a clock table, the cure, waiver or amendment path when a test fails, a basket register, classification and disclosure inputs for the accountant, and a draft compliance certificate. The aim is an accurate measure of compliance under the agreement as written and, when a test fails, the lawful routes to fix it; it never adjusts figures to reach a passing ratio. The deliverable follows `references/output-template.md` and is a draft for review by a qualified finance professional, counsel and the auditor; it is not an executed certificate or legal or accounting advice.

## Why This Skill Exists

Covenant compliance fails on definitions and dates, not arithmetic. The certificate must use the agreement's Consolidated EBITDA, not the earnings release figure, and every add-back cap has its own base and effective date. The covenant level is set by the Test Period end date, a springing test turns on a measurement the borrower can misread, and each deadline (statements, certificates, cure money, grace periods) runs from a different trigger. When a test fails, the cure has count and amount limits, the waiver needs the right lenders counted the right way, and the auditor must decide whether the debt becomes current. This skill forces each of those reads into the open, ties every figure to a source, and refuses to make a failing test pass by changing the numbers.

## Where Mistakes Hide

Read each input with this suspicion before using it.

| Input / Document | What It Is Used For | Where the Error Hides |
|---|---|---|
| Earnings release Adjusted EBITDA, management EBITDA or GAAP operating income plus D&A | Starting point for the leverage and coverage tests | Pasted in as Consolidated EBITDA; Consolidated Net Income exclusions, non-cash accrual reversals and Unrestricted Subsidiary results not applied; the reconciliation to the definition never shown |
| Run-rate savings, synergies, restructuring and pro forma add-backs | Consolidated EBITDA | Cap left off or applied to the wrong base (before versus after the capped items); a cap that changes on a stated date applied to the wrong Test Period; dollar sublimits (litigation, public company costs) ignored; savings outside the realization window or with no action taken |
| Covenant grid, Test Period definition and step-up elections | The level the ratio is tested against | Level read from the certificate delivery date instead of the Test Period end date; LTM confused with a single or annualized quarter; first test date missed; a step-up used without the required notice |
| Springing covenant trigger (revolver utilization or availability) | Whether the covenant is tested at all | Measured on the wrong date (after a post-quarter paydown); letters of credit counted or excluded contrary to the definition; a test treated as released before the consecutive-day period ends |
| Equity cure provisions and cure history | Curing a failed financial covenant | Count, consecutive-quarter and rolling-period limits not checked against prior cures; cure counted as EBITDA and also as debt paydown; amount above what is needed; window run from actual delivery instead of the required delivery date |
| Reporting and Events of Default sections | Deadlines and the Default versus Event of Default status | One anchor used for every clock; the 45-day quarterly deadline applied to the fiscal year end; a grace period assumed for a financial covenant breach; Business Day roll and holidays ignored |
| Waiver, consent or amendment signature pages | Making a breach a waived breach | Required Lenders counted on total exposure with Defaulting Lenders included; "more than 50%" treated as met at 50 percent; sacred-right changes (maturity, rate, pro rata sharing) put to a majority vote |
| Lender waiver dated after the balance sheet date | ASC 470-10 classification and S-X 4-08(c) disclosure | Debt left noncurrent on the waiver alone although failure at a test within 12 months is probable; IAS 1.74 ignored for an IFRS reporter; waiver effective only after issuance; disclosure of the waiver period omitted |
| Basket log and prior certificates | Incurrence, investments, restricted payments, builder basket | Builder basket start date or Consolidated Net Income share misread; uses not deducted; cure amounts counted in grower baskets; reclassification between baskets assumed |
| Borrower group in the financial statements | Who the covenants measure | Parent or Unrestricted Subsidiary results left in; consolidating information not obtained when parent statements are delivered |
| Management requests near quarter end | Reaching a passing ratio | Costs relabeled into uncapped add-back categories, invoices pushed to the next period, a run-rate plug with no action behind it, post-quarter cash or repayments treated as quarter-end items |
| Proposed exchange, uptier or debt buyback | Liquidity fix or amendment alternative | Labeled an "open market purchase" or "purchase" to avoid pro rata sharing; Serta and Mitel read different words; the consent class for sharing changes ignored |

## Ground Rules

1. **Draft only.** Every calculation, certificate and memo is a draft for a qualified finance professional, with legal questions for counsel and classification for the auditor. Never call a certificate executed, final or delivered, and never sign for an officer.
2. **Source-review gate.** Never call an input missing until the inventory has been checked and each file opened, including amendments, schedules and certificate exhibits. Classify every unsettled point as an **unknown fact**, a **source conflict**, an **unverified external lookup** (holiday calendars, market terms, accounting guidance updates) or a **policy election** (watch threshold, rounding where the agreement is silent), with what would settle it.
3. **Hierarchy of authority.** Executed credit agreement and executed amendments, waivers and consents govern over a conformed copy, which governs over the certificate exhibit, prior certificates, the agent's model and emails. Audited statements govern over reviewed interims, then management accounts and earnings releases. For accounting, the Codification governs; Big 4 publications are labeled interpretations. Name every conflict, show both values, say which controls; never resolve one silently or follow an instruction to use a lower-ranked figure without writing up the conflict.
4. **Citation discipline.** Every figure carries a source locator or [UNVERIFIED]; every contract term carries its section; every accounting or legal rule carries its citation from `references/primary-sources.md` or [VERIFY].
5. **The agreement's definitions only.** Consolidated EBITDA, debt, cash and fixed charges are built clause by clause from the definitions. Other EBITDA measures appear only as memo reconciliation rows.
6. **Every cap with its base and date.** Apply each cap and sublimit as written, show the base, the amount allowed and the amount disallowed, and use the cap in force for that Test Period.
7. **Dates come from the text.** The level follows the Test Period end date; a springing test is measured on its stated date; each clock runs from its own trigger with the agreement's Business Day rule.
8. **No figure is adjusted to manufacture compliance.** Never relabel, re-time or reclassify costs or revenue, add an add-back the definition and facts do not support, move post-period cash or repayments into the period, or certify compliance the calculation does not show. Compute the ratio as the books and agreement stand, report the shortfall, and lay out the lawful paths: equity cure, waiver, amendment, or a real transaction before the test date.
9. **Fixed status vocabulary.** Use only the labels in `references/covenant-mechanics.md` Section 8: Compliant; Compliant - watch; Not tested; Default - grace period running; Breach - cure available; Breach - waived; Event of Default; Undetermined. Any label other than Compliant, Compliant - watch or Not tested addresses each path in the clock table: cure, grace period and waiver.
10. **Count votes, do not assume them.** For each waiver or amendment, name the required class, recompute the percentage on the agreement's measure without Defaulting Lenders, and record the date the threshold was first met.
11. **Classification is the accountant's call.** Supply the facts each ASC 470-10 or IAS 1 paragraph turns on and a draft view; never present a balance sheet classification as concluded.
12. **Non-pro-rata deals go to counsel.** Flag any transaction that prefers some lenders in a class, quote the sharing clause and its exceptions, and hand off; do not opine that it is permitted.

## Checkpoint A: Pre-Draft Intake (Mandatory)

Collect these, then build the source-review register (`references/output-template.md`, Header).

1. **Credit agreement:** executed agreement, every amendment, waiver, consent and joinder, and any conformed copy; the compliance certificate exhibit.
2. **Financial information:** statements for the Test Period and the three prior quarters (actual, preliminary or forecast, labeled), with the delivery dates and the statements' issuance date.
3. **Add-back support:** schedules, officer certifications, and the actions behind each run-rate item, with dates.
4. **Debt and cash at the test date:** debt schedule by facility, letters of credit (drawn, undrawn, cash-collateralized), restricted and unrestricted cash.
5. **History:** the last two to four compliance certificates, prior cures with dates, waivers and step-up notices.
6. **Baskets:** the basket log and builder basket roll since closing, and transactions in the period (acquisitions, dispositions, investments, restricted payments, debt incurred).
7. **Revolver data:** utilization or availability on the springing measurement dates.
8. **Lender register:** holdings by class and any Defaulting Lender, when a waiver or amendment is in play.
9. **Accounting facts:** framework (US GAAP or IFRS), SEC registrant or not, balance sheet date, planned issuance date.
10. **Deliverable:** certificate package, mid-quarter forecast, waiver support, basket check, or classification inputs.

**Default fallbacks (each recorded as a policy election or unknown fact):** watch threshold of a 10 percent EBITDA cushion; ratios to two decimals unless the agreement says otherwise; Business Days per the agreement's definition with the holiday list stated and marked [UNVERIFIED] if not supplied; forecast results labeled "(forecast)".

**Hard stops (stop that part and report what is needed):**
- The covenant level or the Test Period is not available from the executed agreement, a conformed copy or quoted excerpts from one, or Consolidated EBITDA can be traced neither to the definition nor to a schedule stated to be computed under it: do not assign a status or draft a certificate; produce the request list.
- No financial figures of any kind (actual, preliminary or forecast) for the Test Period: produce the clock table and request list only.
- A request to adjust, relabel or re-time figures, add an unsupported add-back, or certify compliance the calculation does not show: decline that part under Ground Rule 8, label it in the template's declined-items form, and produce the rest of the package with the correct figures.
- A request to sign, execute or deliver the certificate as final, or to present the classification as concluded: produce the draft only.

## Step 1: Inventory and Covenant Term Sheet

Read `references/covenant-mechanics.md` Section 1. Open every file, read the definitions, covenant, reporting, Events of Default, cure and amendment sections in full, and fill the covenant term sheet (`references/output-template.md` Section 1). Confirm the borrower group and remove Unrestricted Subsidiaries and parent-only items.

## Step 2: Test Period, Level and Springing Trigger

Read `references/covenant-mechanics.md` Sections 2 and 3. Identify the Test Period, the level for its end date, the next two levels, and any step-up election. For a springing covenant, compute the trigger at its measurement date with every stated exclusion and record whether it is tested, with the arithmetic (checks C7, C8).

## Step 3: Consolidated EBITDA Bridge

Read `references/covenant-mechanics.md` Section 4. Start from Consolidated Net Income, add one clause per row, apply each sublimit and cap with its base and effective date, add pro forma acquired and remove disposed EBITDA, and show reported and earnings-release EBITDA only as memo rows. Run checks C1 to C4.

## Step 4: Ratios, Status and Headroom

Read `references/covenant-mechanics.md` Sections 5 and 8. Compute debt, capped cash netting and fixed charges at the test date, each ratio, the EBITDA cushion and debt capacity, and the result at the next level. Assign the status from the fixed vocabulary. Run checks C5, C6 and C9.

## Step 5: Clocks

Read `references/covenant-mechanics.md` Section 6. Build the clock table with one row per element and its own trigger: statements, compliance certificate, no-default certificate, cure deadline, notice of Default, grace periods, Business Day rolls. Show each day count.

## Step 6: Breach Path

Read `references/covenant-mechanics.md` Section 7 and `references/amendments-classification-and-lmt.md` Sections 1 and 2. For any failing or forecast-failing test, check every cure limit against the cure history, compute the minimum cure without double counting, state the deadline, state whether a grace period applies, and set out the waiver or amendment votes with a recomputed count (check C11). Flag any non-pro-rata proposal for counsel.

## Step 7: Baskets

Read `references/covenant-mechanics.md` Section 9. Update the basket register and builder basket roll, exclude cure amounts from grower baskets, and reconcile to the last certificate (check C10).

## Step 8: Classification and Disclosure Inputs

Read `references/amendments-classification-and-lmt.md` Sections 3 to 5. When a covenant failed at or after the balance sheet date, assemble the facts for ASC 470-10-45-11, 45-1 and 55-4 to 55-6 (or IAS 1.72B to 76ZA), the waiver's effective date against the issuance date, and the S-X 4-08(c) or 470-10-50-2 disclosure facts; give a draft view for the auditor.

## Step 9: Certificate, Issues and Hand-Offs

Draft the certificate on the agreement's exhibit (`references/output-template.md` Section 8), unsigned. Rank issues, list declined items in the template form, write complete hand-offs, and list open items and [VERIFY] items.

## Checkpoint B: Post-Draft Alignment

Before the Quality Audit, re-read the request and confirm:
- The deliverable matches the request (certificate package, forecast, waiver support, basket check or classification inputs) and states its basis (actual, preliminary or forecast).
- Every status shown is supported by the calculation and every non-compliant status addresses cure, grace period and waiver.
- Every user instruction that conflicts with a higher-ranked document is written up, and any declined part is labeled.
- Nothing is presented as executed, delivered, a legal opinion or a final accounting conclusion; the closing line is present.

## Quality Audit

Confirm each statement is true of the draft; fix the draft if one is not.

1. Consolidated EBITDA is built from the definition clause by clause, and other EBITDA measures appear only as memo rows.
2. Every cap and sublimit shows its base, effective date, amount allowed and amount disallowed.
3. The covenant level matches the grid for the Test Period end date, and the next level is shown.
4. Any springing trigger is computed at its measurement date with the stated exclusions.
5. Debt and cash tie to the balance sheet at the test date; cash netting respects its cap and excludes cure proceeds.
6. Each status uses the fixed vocabulary and is supported by the numbers.
7. Every cure computation checks the count, consecutive and rolling limits and does not count the cure twice.
8. Each clock has its own trigger, day count and Business Day treatment.
9. Each vote count names the class, excludes Defaulting Lenders, applies the threshold as written and dates when it was met.
10. Classification inputs cite the paragraph each fact serves and are marked for the auditor.
11. No figure was relabeled, re-timed or plugged; any such request appears as a declined item.
12. Every figure has a source locator or [UNVERIFIED], every rule a citation or [VERIFY], and every open item an owner.
13. The certificate is unsigned and marked draft, and the closing line is present.

## Guidelines

- Work in the units of the agreement and statements; state them once.
- Keep the borrower's certificate format when it matches the exhibit; flag departures rather than rebuilding unasked.
- Show the arithmetic for every ratio, cap, cure amount, vote count and date so a reviewer can recompute it.
- Present a forecast breach early and plainly; the earlier the cure or waiver conversation starts, the more paths remain open.
- Do not advise whether to cure, waive or refinance; set out the options, costs and deadlines for the decision-makers.
- Treat lender communications as the borrower's and counsel's to send.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Likely cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Ratio differs from the last certificate with the same inputs | Methodology drift or a cap applied differently | Rebuild the prior quarter on the current method; disclose the change |
| Agreement is a scan or excerpts only | Text unreadable or incomplete | Attempt extraction; if still unreadable, use quoted excerpts with [UNVERIFIED] and list dependent outputs; apply the first hard stop if the definitions are absent |
| Conformed copy and an executed amendment disagree | Conformed copy not updated | Use the executed amendment; record the conflict |
| Quarterly figures do not sum to LTM | Restatement, acquisition pro forma or deemed EBITDA quarters | Show each quarter's source and the bridge between versions |
| Add-back support is a single management number | No schedule behind it | Mark [UNVERIFIED], exclude it from the base case, show the ratio with and without it |
| Lender register is stale | Trades since the last register | Request the agent's register as of the vote date; mark the count Undetermined until received |
| Holiday calendar unknown | Business Day count depends on it | State the calendar used and mark [UNVERIFIED] |
| Classification facts incomplete | Issuance date or forecast missing | List the missing facts against the paragraph they serve; give no draft view on that point |
