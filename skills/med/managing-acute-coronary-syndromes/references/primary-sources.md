## 3. Changes from the previous version of this skill

| Item | Before | After | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Governing guideline | 2014 NSTE-ACS and 2021 revascularization guidelines | 2025 ACS guideline with its three corrections | 2025 guideline |
| MI definition | Not named | Fourth UDMI and Fifth UDMI (2026) side by side | UDMI texts |
| Door-in-door-out and fibrinolysis "within 30 minutes" | Stated as guideline benchmarks | Measure targets (PM-7, PM-10, CMS996v6); the eCQM transfer criterion is 45 minutes from ED arrival | 2017 PMs; CMS996v6 |
| New LBBB | Listed as STEMI criterion | Not a STEMI equivalent alone; Sgarbossa criteria | 2025 Table 3; 2022 ECDP |

| DAPT | "minimum 12 months" | At least 1 year if not high bleeding risk, with Class 1 and 2b bleeding-reduction options | 2025, 11.1 |
