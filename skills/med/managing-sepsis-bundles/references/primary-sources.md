## 2. Confirmed statements

| Statement | Where |
|---|---|
| v5.18a governs 01/01/2026-12/31/2026 inpatient discharges | QualityNet manual listing |
| SEP-1 is a 3-hour and 6-hour bundle: lactate, blood cultures before antibiotics, antibiotics within 3 hours; repeat lactate within 6 hours when initial lactate is over 2; 30 mL/kg crystalloid for hypotension or lactate of 4 or more; vasopressors for persistent hypotension; repeat volume status and tissue perfusion assessment | CMS SEP-1 measure descriptions and QRC training |
| Severe sepsis presentation time is when the last of infection, 2 or more SIRS and organ dysfunction is met, or earlier provider documentation of severe sepsis | QRC Q&A; JAMA Netw Open SEP-1 cohort methods |
| Crystalloid window runs from 6 hours before through 3 hours after the earlier of initial hypotension and septic shock presentation; ordered volume within 10 percent below 30 mL/kg accepted; the 10 percent rule does not apply to the infused volume | QRC August 2024 v5.16 training |
| Initial lactate window: 6 hours before through 3 hours after severe sepsis presentation | QRC material; published SEP-1 methods; vendor SEP-1 data definitions |
| Persistent hypotension: two consecutive hypotensive readings in the hour after the target volume is infused | QRC composite-measure training |
| Initial hypotension: two hypotensive readings, not necessarily consecutive, within 3 hours of each other | QRC Q&A (v5.5a era) |
| Antibiotic: one monotherapy-table agent, or agents from two classes on the combination table, both started within 3 hours | QRC Q&A |
| Exclusions: comfort or palliative care directive within 6 hours; administrative contraindication; transfer in from acute care facility or ASC; sepsis clinical trial; discharge within 6 hours of presentation | QRC 2022 algorithm overview; 2016 update webinar |
| COVID-19 exclusion removed from the Initial Patient Population in v5.18 | Listserve 2025-66-IP |
| Reassessment may be met by physician, APN or PA attestation (introduced v5.2a) | QRC Q&A |
| IBW may set the target volume when a provider documents obesity (BMI over 30) | QRC v5.8 training |
| Sepsis-3 definitions, SOFA 2 or more, qSOFA thresholds, septic shock criteria | Singer 2016 |
| Hour-1 bundle elements and time zero | Levy 2018 |
| SSC 2026: within 1 hour for possible, probable or definite septic shock and for probable or definite sepsis without shock (recommend); 3 hours for possible sepsis without shock; at least 30 mL/kg in first 3 hours (suggest); balanced crystalloids; serial lactate; capillary refill adjunct; norepinephrine first line; vasopressin added on escalating norepinephrine; against terlipressin; IV corticosteroids (suggest); against qSOFA as single screen | SCCM 2026 guideline page and summaries |
