# Output Template

Produce the header and the sections below in order. Two modes share the template: clinical drafting support (a clinician completing documentation for an encounter they conducted) and chart or quality review (a reviewer checking documentation after the fact). Every finding names its source. Put detail in tables; keep narrative short.

## Contents

1. Header and crisis line
2. Section 1. Summary
3. Section 2. Source register and encounter ledger
4. Section 3. Population, standard and tool fit
5. Section 4. Screen interpretation
6. Section 5. Assessment content
7. Section 6. Formulation scaffold (for the clinician to complete)
8. Section 7. Safety plan and lethal means review
9. Section 8. Transition and follow-up
10. Section 9. Reconciliation table
11. Section 10. Gaps and hand-offs
12. Section 11. Open items and `[VERIFY]` list

## Header and crisis line

- Mode: clinical drafting support, or chart or quality review.
- Setting, accreditation program and the citation in force (for example "Hospital program, NPG.08.01.01, effective January 1, 2026").
- Encounter date and time; patient age; primary reason for care (de-identified).
- Preparer note: "Draft for review by a qualified clinician. Not a final risk determination and not an order."
- Crisis line, always printed: "If anyone is in immediate danger, call 911 or local emergency services. The 988 Suicide and Crisis Lifeline is available 24/7 by call or text to 988 or chat at 988lifeline.org."

## 9. Reconciliation table

| # | Fact | Source A says | Source B says | Controlling source and why | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Typical rows: screen color band against the documented level; copied note against a same-day nursing note; "denies access to firearms" against a collateral report of a gun in the home; older ASQ refusal rule against the 9/29/25 tool.

## 10. Gaps and hand-offs

| Gap | Element affected | Gap class (unknown fact / source conflict / unverified external lookup / policy election) | Who can resolve | Compliant next step |
|---|---|---|---|---|

| To skill | Why | What is passed | What this draft did not do |
|---|---|---|---|
| managing-psychiatric-emergencies | Acute positive screen or imminent risk needing emergency evaluation | Screen, ledger | Did not evaluate or direct care |
| managing-involuntary-commitments | Hold criteria or state law questions | Facts documented | Did not apply state law |
| coordinating-care-transitions | Follow-up and hand-off gaps | Transition table | Did not schedule care |
