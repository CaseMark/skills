Produce the header and the parts below in order. One row per arrangement and per period in which terms changed. Every date shows its weekday. Quote contract terms only from executed documents, with section and page. Keep the narrative short and put detail in tables.

## Header

- Organization and DHS entities in scope; review period; today's date; requester and role (compliance officer, internal audit, contract administrator).
- Purpose: routine portfolio review, targeted review of named arrangements, renewal or expiration sweep, limits check, or pre-escalation fact assembly.
- Preparer note: "Draft for compliance officer and health care regulatory counsel review. Not a legal opinion."

**Review status**, one label used exactly:

- **Review complete: no escalations**
- **Review complete: escalations listed**
- **Incomplete: inputs missing** (name each input and who supplies it)
- **Declined: hard stop** (name the request declined, why, and what the review did instead)

A declined request does not stop the rest of the review; the status of the rest is given on its own line.

## Part 1. Finding labels and summary

One label per arrangement and period, used exactly:

- **Documented and performing to terms**: signed writing (or a documented reason none is needed), term or holdover tracked, FMV support on file, and payments and performance match the writing.
- **Gap: correct within the rules**: a fix the regulation allows is still open (a signature inside the 90-day window, an unintended payment discrepancy during the term, a return under 411.357(k)(3) within its deadline). Name the fix and its deadline.
- **Potential noncompliance: escalate to counsel**: a trigger in the escalation list is present and no cure in the clock table is open. State the facts, the period and each cure considered; do not state a legal conclusion. A request to relabel or withhold this finding is a hard stop.
- **Cannot determine**: name the missing fact and who can supply it.
- **Outside this review**: no physician or immediate family member party, or a question that belongs to another skill; name the route.

Add "provisional pending <facts>" where an unchecked fact could change the label. Then one short paragraph per arrangement with the label, the main reason and the next action.
