## 2. Confirmed statements

| Statement | Where |
|---|---|
| PY 2026 and PY 2027 use 100% of the 2024 CMS-HCC model for non-PACE MA; V28 is fully phased in | CY 2026 and CY 2027 Rate Announcements |
| Diagnoses must come from a face-to-face visit with an acceptable provider type at an acceptable source; diagnostic radiology is unacceptable | MMCM ch. 7; PY 2024 RADV methods |
| Audio-video telehealth qualifies for MA; audio-only does not, and 93/FQ-only records are excluded from 2027 risk scores | HPMS memo May 4, 2022; CY 2027 Rate Announcement; July 9, 2026 memo |
| Plans must delete submitted codes that fail requirements; overpayments reported and returned within 60 days of identification | MMCM ch. 7, §40; 42 CFR 422.326 |
| 2023 RADV rule vacated; appeal pending; CMS has not decided on extrapolated collection for PY 2020 and PY 2024 | Humana opinion; PY 2020 and PY 2024 methods |
| Uncertain diagnoses are not coded in the outpatient setting; previously treated conditions that no longer exist are not coded | ICD-10-CM FY 2027, IV.H, IV.J |
| HHS-HCC models are concurrent and HHS accepts certain audio-only codes | 2027 Payment Notice; HHS telehealth FAQ |
