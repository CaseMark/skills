## 2. Confirmed statements

| Statement | Where |
|---|---|
| Medicare FFS windows run from receipt of the notice, presumed 5 days after its date; the request is filed when received | 405.942, 405.962, 405.1002, 405.1102, 405.1136 |
| 2026 thresholds: $200 ALJ, $1,960 court | 90 FR 55869; CMS appeal pages |
| A RAC may not perform clinical validation and may not look back more than 3 years from the claim paid date | RAC SOW 2023 and 2025 |
| ADR response: 45 days (MAC, RAC, SMRC, CERT); 30 days (UPIC) | PIM ch. 3, 3.2.3.2 |
| Recoupment cannot begin before day 41 and stops on a timely redetermination request | 405.379(d)(1) |
| Institutional timely filing runs from the "Through" date | Claims Processing Manual ch. 1, 70.1 |
| Part B inpatient claims after a Part A denial follow the 1-year limit | 414.5(c); Benefit Policy Manual ch. 6, 10.1 |
| Hospital DRG review request: 60 days from the DRG notice; no revision after | 412.60(d) |
| MA reconsideration: 60 days after presumed receipt (5 days), so 65 days from the notice; non-contract providers file a waiver of liability; contract provider payment disputes follow the contract | 422.582(b); CMS appeals guidance (July 6, 2026) |
| MA plans follow 412.3 and may use internal criteria only under 422.101(b)(6) | 422.101; CMS FAQ (Feb. 6, 2024) |
| An approved prior authorization or concurrent determination cannot later be denied for medical necessity absent good cause or fraud reopening | 422.138(c) |
| Code assignment rests on the provider's diagnostic statement, not clinical criteria | Guidelines I.A.19 |
| Sepsis-3 organ dysfunction is a SOFA change of 2 or more; baseline zero absent known preexisting dysfunction | Singer 2016, Box 3 and Table 1 |
