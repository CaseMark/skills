## 1. Medicare fee-for-service clock rules

- Receipt is presumed 5 calendar days after the date on the notice, unless there is evidence to the contrary: redetermination (42 CFR 405.942(a)(1)), reconsideration (405.962(a)(1)), ALJ (405.1002(a)(3)), Council (405.1102(a)(2)), court (405.1136(c)(2)).
- A request is filed on the date the contractor, QIC, OMHA office or Council receives it (405.942(a)(2), 405.962(a)(2), 405.1002(a)(4), 405.1102(a)(3)).
- The date a denials team, coder or business office first saw the letter is not evidence of when the provider received it. Evidence to the contrary is a dated mailroom log, envelope or portal receipt showing a later receipt by the party.
- Weekends and holidays: the regulations above contain no roll provision. Report the weekday of every deadline and target receipt by the prior business day when the deadline falls on a weekend or holiday; for contracts, apply only a roll provision the contract states.
- A late request needs a written good-cause showing (405.942(b)); the examples are illness, destroyed records, misleading contractor information, non-receipt of the notice, or a timely filing sent to the wrong government agency. Internal workload is not on the list.

## 2. Medicare fee-for-service appeal levels

| Level | Decided by | Filing window and its trigger | Decision time (MLN006562, Sept. 2025) | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Redetermination | MAC | 120 days after receipt of the initial or revised determination notice (405.942) | 60 days | No amount in controversy |
| 2. Reconsideration | QIC | 180 days after receipt of the redetermination notice (405.962) | 60 days; each later evidence submission extends it up to 14 days (405.966(b)) | Provider must submit all evidence, including what the redetermination notice said was missing, before the QIC decides; absent good cause, later evidence is precluded (405.966(a)) |
| 3. ALJ hearing or attorney adjudicator (OMHA) | OMHA | 60 days after receipt of the QIC notice (405.1002(a)); escalation if the QIC is late (405.1002(b)) | 90 days; 180 days after escalation | New provider evidence is excluded unless good cause under 405.1028 |
| 4. Council review | Medicare Appeals Council (DAB) | 60 days after receipt of the ALJ decision or dismissal (405.1102(a)) | 90 days; 180 days after escalation | |
| 5. Judicial review | U.S. District Court | 60 days after receipt of the Council decision (405.1136(c); CMS fifth-level appeal page) | No statutory limit | Venue: district of residence or principal place of business (405.1136(b)) |

## 3. Amount in controversy

| Calendar year of the request | ALJ hearing | Judicial review | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2026 (requests filed Jan. 1-Dec. 31, 2026) | $200 | $1,960 | CMS-4209-N, 90 FR 55869 (Dec. 4, 2025); CMS appeal pages |
| 2027 | $200 | $2,000 | CMS third- and fifth-level appeal pages; Federal Register notice not read `[VERIFY]` |

- The threshold that applies is the one in force on the date the request is filed, not the date of service.
- Amount in controversy is the amount charged less Medicare payments already made and collectible deductible and coinsurance (405.1006(d)(1)); for an overpayment it is the demanded amount, or the extrapolated amount when sampling was used (405.1006(d)(4)). Claims may be aggregated when they share common issues of law and fact (405.1006(a)).

## 4. Review contractors and their limits

| Contractor | Role | Documentation window | Limits that matter in an appeal |
|---|---|---|---|
| MAC | Pre- and post-payment medical review, DRG validation, redeterminations | 45 days to answer an ADR (PIM ch. 3, 3.2.3.2; 405.929, 405.930) | DRG validation follows ICD coding guidelines, UHDDS definitions and CMS clarifications, and may not institute new coding requirements (PIM ch. 6, 6.5.3) |
| RAC | Post-payment recovery audit, contingency fee | 45 days to answer an ADR | No review more than 3 years after the initial claim paid date, measured to the ADR date (complex) or review results letter (automated); clinical validation is prohibited in all RAC reviews; automated review needs certainty plus a written Medicare policy or sanctioned coding guideline (RAC SOW Region 2, Feb. 2023; Regions 3-5, Apr. 2025) |
| RAC discussion period | Informal exchange before adjustment | 30 days after the review results letter | Not an appeal level; the redetermination clock runs from the MAC's notice of the revised determination `[VERIFY]` |
| UPIC | Program integrity, fraud investigation | 30 days to answer an ADR (PIM ch. 3, 3.2.3.2) | Potentially fraudulent entries found by any contractor are referred to the UPIC (PIM ch. 3, 3.3.2.5) |
| CERT, SMRC | Error-rate testing, special reviews | 45 days (CERT, SMRC) | Same appeal rights as other revised determinations |

A RAC finding that rests on whether the patient truly had a documented condition is clinical validation and is outside the RAC's scope; say so in the appeal and cite the SOW. Whether MACs, UPICs or QIOs may perform clinical validation is not stated in PIM 6.5.3 `[VERIFY]`.

## 5. Recoupment, reopening, adjustment and rebilling clocks

| Clock | Trigger | Window | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Recoupment start | Date of the initial overpayment demand letter | Contractor may recoup no earlier than day 41; a timely, valid redetermination request stops or prevents recoupment | 405.379(d)(1) |
| Recoupment after redetermination | Date of the redetermination notice (affirmed) | Resumes on day 60 unless a timely, valid reconsideration request is received | 405.379(e) |
| Reopening at the provider's request | Date of the initial determination or redetermination | 1 year for any reason; 4 years for good cause; clerical error at any time | 405.980(c) |

| Part B inpatient claim after a Part A denial for an unnecessary admission | Date of service: the "Through" date on an institutional claim | 1 calendar year after the date of service | 414.5(c); 424.44(a)(1); Claims Processing Manual ch. 1, 70.1; Benefit Policy Manual ch. 6, 10.1 |

An appeal of the Part A denial does not extend the Part B window. Whether a hospital may file the Part B inpatient claim while the Part A appeal is pending, and whether it must withdraw the appeal first, is `[VERIFY]` under current instructions (CMS-1455-R of 2013 allowed withdrawal or 180 days after an adverse decision; that ruling was transitional). Analyze both readings and put the election to the hospital before the Part B window closes.

## 6. Medicare Advantage

- Organization determinations include post-service payment denials and decisions made concurrently with the stay (422.566(b)(3)). Parties: the enrollee, an assignee provider who waives payment from the enrollee, the estate, or another provider found to have an appealable interest (422.574).
- Standard reconsideration: written request to the plan within 60 calendar days after receipt of the written organization determination; receipt presumed 5 calendar days after the notice date; filed when the plan or its named delegate receives it (422.582(b)). Counted from the notice date, that is 65 days.
- Non-contract provider: CMS guidance (effective July 6, 2026) says the remittance or notice must tell the provider it has 65 calendar days from the remittance notification date and must submit a waiver of liability holding the enrollee harmless; the plan should seek a missing waiver before dismissing.
- Contract provider: no party may appeal a contract provider payment denial when the enrollee has no further financial liability; the dispute follows the provider agreement's dispute provisions (same guidance). Read the agreement for its window and levels.
- Plan decision time on a payment reconsideration: 60 days from receipt; an affirmed denial is auto-forwarded to the independent review entity (422.590(b)); missing the deadline counts as an affirmation (422.590(d)). Later levels: ALJ (generally 60 days after receipt of the IRE notice, per the CMS guidance citing 422.602), Council, court; the MA amount-in-controversy rule (422.600) was not read `[VERIFY]`.
- Prior authorization or concurrent approval: a coordinated care plan that approved the item or service may not later deny it for lack of medical necessity, and may reopen only for good cause or reliable evidence of fraud or similar fault (422.138(c), as amended April 15, 2025).
- Peer-to-peer: voluntary; plans may not use it to solicit a lower level of service; coverage decisions remain initial determinations subject to notice and appeal (CMS guidance, July 6, 2026). It does not replace a written reconsideration request.

## 7. Commercial, ERISA and other payers

- The provider agreement and the payer's provider manual set the appeal levels, windows, trigger dates (letter date, receipt date or remittance date), submission channel and any clinical definitions. Never import the Medicare 5-day presumption or Medicare levels into a commercial contract.
- ERISA group health plans must give claimants at least 180 days after receipt of an adverse benefit determination to appeal (29 CFR 2560.503-1(h)(3)(i)), and may not bar an authorized representative (2560.503-1(b)(4)). The claimant is the participant; a provider appeals under the plan's procedure only as an authorized representative or as the contract allows.
- State external review, prompt-pay and Medicaid managed care appeal rules vary by state `[VERIFY]` for the state at issue.
