## 3. Corrections to the earlier version of this skill

| Item | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Edoxaban dose reduction | "CrCl 15-50, weight 60 kg or less, or P-gp inhibitor" applied to all indications | That rule is for DVT/PE; NVAF reduces for CrCl 15 to 50 only and not for P-gp inhibitors; do not use when CrCl > 95 |
| Apixaban and rivaroxaban reversal | "Andexanet alfa or 4F-PCC" | Andexanet no longer sold in the U.S. after December 22, 2025; 4F-PCC is off-label for factor Xa inhibitors with a Class 1 (C-LD) AF guideline recommendation |
| Bridging threshold | "CHA2DS2-VASc 1-4 low; 7 or more high" with enoxaparin bridging dose | CHEST 2022 Table 1 categories with sourcing; AF on warfarin: strong recommendation against bridging; no dose values in the controller |
| DOAC holds | "24-48 hours / 48-72 hours" by bleed risk | Days-off counting per drug and CrCl from CHEST 2022 and AF guideline Table 18, plus label minimums |
| Joint Commission | NPSG.03.05.01 | NPG.14.04.01 for Hospital and CAH from January 1, 2026 |
| Unsourced claims | ISMP ranking, ED visit statistic, TTR 65 percent target, heparin nomogram doses | Removed; not verified in a primary source. Weight-based nomograms stay facility policy elections |
| Enoxaparin and heparin | "Reduce to 1 mg/kg daily if CrCl < 30"; "80 units/kg bolus, 18 units/kg/hour" | Lovenox Table 1 regimens and heparin label 2.2 and 2.3 content, read in full (fix pass) |
