| Contractions mean true labor unless a physician, CNM or QMP certifies false labor after reasonable observation | Reg (b); App. V |
| Good-faith inpatient admission ends the hospital's EMTALA duty; admission to avoid EMTALA does not | Reg (d)(2); App. V, A-2407 |
