### Date of first sale

The form's definition, quoted in full: the date of first sale is "the date on which the first investor is irrevocably contractually committed to invest, which, depending on the terms and conditions of the contract, could be the date on which the issuer receives the investor's subscription agreement or check" (Form D General Instructions p. 7; Item 7 instruction p. 10; Securities Act Rules C&DI 257.02).

Applied to the earliest subscription for the only vehicle:

1. **Signing and delivery.** Meridian Teachers' Retirement System signed on September 14, 2026 (Monday) (06a, p. 14, Carol Nakamura, Executive Director). It was the earliest investor signature.
2. **Revocability.** Subscription Agreement s. 2 (p. 2): "The Investor may withdraw this subscription by written notice at any time prior to the General Partner's acceptance. Upon acceptance by the General Partner, evidenced by its countersignature on the signature page, this subscription shall be irrevocable and the Investor shall be bound to make the Capital Commitment set forth on the signature page." The investor could withdraw until the GP countersigned.
3. **Dependence on a later act of the issuer.** Yes: acceptance by countersignature, in the GP's sole discretion (s. 2). LPA s. 3.3 (p. 13) confirms that admission occurs on the GP's acceptance "as evidenced by the General Partner's countersignature".

Result: the Meridian subscription became irrevocable on the GP's countersignature, dated **September 16, 2026 (Wednesday)** (06a, p. 14, Dana Whitcombe, Managing Member). That is the date of first sale. The contract is not silent or ambiguous, so there is no alternative candidate date under the ambiguity rule; the two other dates in the matter are rejected for the reasons below and the conflict is written up in Section 2, issue 4.

- September 14, 2026 (Monday), the investor's signature date: rejected, because the subscription was revocable until acceptance.
- September 18, 2026 (Friday), the "Initial Closing Date" used by the closing memo (09, p. 1) and the administrator's Notes sheet (08): rejected, because a closing label is not the contractual event. Four of the five subscriptions were accepted on September 18, but Meridian's was accepted two days earlier. Capital will first be called in October 2026 (09, p. 1), which confirms that payment is not the test.

### Amendment triggers to calendar (Rule 503(a)(3))

No amendment is due now. Once the new notice is filed:

- **Annual amendment:** on or before the first anniversary of the filing date, if the offering is still continuing (the offering window runs up to twelve months after the Initial Closing Date, LPA s. 3.5 (p. 13); PPM p. 7). The anniversary resets with each filed amendment.
- **Change-driven amendments, as soon as practicable after the change:** any later closing that adds a new related person or a new sales compensation recipient (never excepted); any change of exemption or Section 3(c) paragraph; any increase in the total offering amount of more than 10 percent cumulatively (on a $250,000,000 baseline, more than $25,000,000; an increase to exactly $275,000,000 is 10.0 percent, not "more than 10 percent", and would be excepted if the change occurred solely in excepted information); any decrease in the minimum investment of more than 10 percent cumulatively; any non-accredited investor count above 35 (none expected in a 3(c)(7) fund).
