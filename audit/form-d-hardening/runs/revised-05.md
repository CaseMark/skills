**Date of first sale: contract reasoning.** The Form D instruction defines the date of first sale as the date on which the first investor is irrevocably contractually committed to invest, which, depending on the terms and conditions of the contract, could be the date on which the issuer receives the investor's subscription agreement or check. The Subscription Agreement (05, s. 2, p. 2) provides: "The Investor may withdraw this subscription by written notice at any time prior to the General Partner's acceptance. Upon acceptance by the General Partner, evidenced by its countersignature on the signature page, this subscription shall be irrevocable and the Investor shall be bound to make the Capital Commitment set forth on the signature page." LPA s. 3.3 (p. 13) likewise makes admission turn on the General Partner's countersignature. The subscription is therefore revocable until acceptance, and the date of first sale is the earliest countersignature date. The earliest is Meridian Teachers' Retirement System: investor signature September 14, 2026 (Monday), General Partner countersignature September 16, 2026 (Wednesday), by Dana Whitcombe (06a, p. 14). The other four subscriptions were countersigned September 18, 2026 (Friday). Capital is first called in October 2026 (09, p. 1); payment is not the test.

The closing memo (09, p. 1) and the administrator's Notes sheet (08, sheet "Notes") both state that the date of first sale is the Initial Closing Date, September 18, 2026.
