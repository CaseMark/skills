**Date of first sale: the form's definition and the contract reasoning.** The Form D instructions define the date of first sale as "the date on which the first investor is irrevocably contractually committed to invest, which, depending on the terms and conditions of the contract, could be the date on which the issuer receives the investor's subscription agreement or check" (Form D General Instructions p. 7; Item 7 instruction p. 10; Securities Act Rules C&DI 257.02). The subscription agreement used by all three vehicles (05 s. 2, p. 2; 04s p. 2 confirms Feeder investors use the Feeder's own agreement on the same form) provides: "The Investor may withdraw this subscription by written notice at any time prior to the General Partner's acceptance. Upon acceptance by the General Partner, evidenced by its countersignature on the signature page, this subscription shall be irrevocable." LPA s. 3.3 (02 p. 13; same in 02p and 02f) admits the limited partner on the GP's acceptance as evidenced by countersignature. The subscription is therefore revocable until acceptance, and the governing event is the GP's countersignature date on the executed signature page, not the investor's signature date, the scheduled closing date, the capital call (first call October 2026 per 09 p. 1), or the date in the closing memo or register.

## 4. Judgment calls

**JC 1. One Form D or three (client question in 12).** Options: (A) three separate new notices, one per issuer; (B) one co-issuer notice with the Main Fund as primary issuer and III-A and the Feeder on Items 1 and 2 continuation pages (permitted for issuers that sold in the same Regulation D offering; a master fund and its feeders are the staff's example, Forms C&DI 130.01-130.02). The facts support treating the three vehicles as one Regulation D offering: one PPM and supplement, one GP and Investment Manager, a shared cap, common terms, overlapping sales (02, 02p, 02f, 04, 04s). Either structure is available. **Recommendation: (A), three separate notices.** Reasons: (1) deadline risk: under (B) the single date of first sale is September 16, 2026 and the single due date is October 1, 2026 for all three issuers, and all three CIKs would be needed by then, while under (A) the Parallel Fund and Feeder keep their October 5 due dates; (2) Item 14 has no clarification field, and a co-issuer count must choose between 10 ultimate investors and 11 names (the Feeder being both a co-issuer and an investor in the Main Fund), which a reader cannot be told; (3) each issuer's Items 13, 14 and 15 stay on its own notice without a program-wide number in an issuer field; (4) several states require a notice per issuer in any event (routed to `blue-sky-filings`). What changes under (B): one filing, one deadline (October 1), multiple-issuer box checked, Main Fund primary, two continuation pages; Item 13(a) $250,000,000, 13(b) $156,000,000 (Feeder counted once), 13(c) $94,000,000; Item 14 would be 10 (ultimate investors, excluding the Feeder as an investor) or 11, a judgment call `[VERIFY]`; Item 15 $900,000 Estimate (or $1,500,000 under the Northgate alternative); one Rule 506(b)/3(c)(7) selection for all; one signature with the duly-authorized-representative statement for the other issuers. Structure (A) also preserves the fallback of filing the Main Fund alone on October 1 if only its CIK has issued. The option of adding the new vehicles to an existing notice by amendment does not exist because no notice has been filed.

**JC 2. Item 11, minimum investment (no clarification field; required every time).** For each issuer the four questions:
1. Stated minimum: Main Fund $5,000,000 (02 s. 3.2 p. 12; 04 p. 7, agree); Parallel Fund $5,000,000 (02p s. 3.2; 04s and 04 p. 7); Feeder $1,000,000 (02f s. 3.2; the PPM supplement does not state a different figure).
2. Waiver authority: the GP "may, in its sole discretion, accept a Capital Commitment of a lesser amount" (02 s. 3.2; 02p s. 3.2; 02f s. 3.2 "discretion to accept less"); no condition, no class limitation, no advisory committee consent; the authority sits in the LPA and is not shown to be changeable only with investor consent.
3. Non-waivable floor: none in any LPA, the PPM or the supplement; no side letter or written policy is in the matter.
4. Accepted below-minimum subscriptions: Main Fund, Whitcombe Family Trust $1,000,000 (06e p. 14), accepted by Priya Raghunathan; the trustee, Dana Whitcombe, is a Managing Member of the GP (03 Schedule A), so the trust is not an outside investor (family members and controlled entities of officers and general partners are excluded) and the acceptance does not set the Item 11 figure. Parallel Fund: none below $5,000,000. Feeder: none below $1,000,000 (lowest $4,000,000).
Readings: (i) unrestricted discretion with no floor anywhere: report 0 under Forms C&DI 130.03 `[VERIFY]` (recommended for all three issuers); (ii) a policy floor the issuer confirms in writing it will not waive below for outside investors: report that floor; (iii) the stated minimum ($5,000,000 / $5,000,000 / $1,000,000) if the issuer confirms it will accept nothing less from any outside investor. **Recommendation: 0 for each issuer**, with the issuer asked to confirm that no written floor exists.
