### Date of first sale: the contractual reasoning

The Form D instruction defines the date of first sale as "the date on which the first investor is irrevocably contractually committed to invest, which, depending on the terms and conditions of the contract, could be the date on which the issuer receives the investor's subscription agreement or check."

Subscription Agreement s. 2 (Acceptance; Revocability, p. 2) provides that the investor "may withdraw this subscription by written notice at any time prior to the General Partner's acceptance", that "[u]pon acceptance by the General Partner, evidenced by its countersignature on the signature page, this subscription shall be irrevocable", and that the General Partner may accept or reject in its sole discretion. Main Fund LPA s. 3.3 (p. 13) confirms that admission occurs on the General Partner's acceptance, as evidenced by countersignature. The investor is therefore not irrevocably committed on signing or delivery; it is committed on the General Partner's countersignature. The governing event is the acceptance date on each executed signature page, not the closing date in the closing memo or the "Closing date" column of the register.

**Main Fund.** Acceptance dates: Meridian September 16, 2026 (Wednesday); Covewood, Hallam, Sorrel Point and Whitcombe Family Trust September 18, 2026 (Friday). The earliest is Meridian's, September 16, 2026. The closing memo (09, p. 1) and the register "Notes" sheet (08) state "first sale date = initial closing date 9/18/2026". That is a conflict between rank-3 sources and the executed subscription (rank 1); the executed subscription controls. Using September 18 would set the due date two business days late (October 5 instead of October 1). Capital is first called in October 2026 (closing memo p. 1); payment is not the test.

**Parallel Fund.** Both acceptances are dated September 18, 2026 (Friday). The Northgate subscription was signed September 16, 2026, but under s. 2 the investor could withdraw until acceptance, so the date of first sale is September 18, 2026. The memo assumes the Parallel Fund subscriptions (06f, 06l) are on the same 14-page form as 05 with the same s. 2; if either were instead irrevocable on delivery, the date of first sale would be September 16, 2026 (Northgate's signature) and the due date October 1, 2026. See Section 6, assumption 2. Because the Main Fund must file by October 1 in any event, the practical recommendation is to file both notices on or before October 1, 2026 (Thursday), which removes the risk from this assumption.
