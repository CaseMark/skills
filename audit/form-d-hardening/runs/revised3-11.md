**Date of first sale: the definition.** The Form D General Instructions define the date of first sale as "the date on which the first investor is irrevocably contractually committed to invest, which, depending on the terms and conditions of the contract, could be the date on which the issuer receives the investor's subscription agreement or check" (Form D General Instructions p. 7 and Item 7 instruction p. 10; Securities Act Rules C&DI 257.02).

**Contract reasoning.** Each executed subscription incorporates the form's s. 2 (Acceptance; Revocability, p. 2): the investor "may withdraw this subscription by written notice at any time prior to the General Partner's acceptance," and "upon acceptance by the General Partner, evidenced by its countersignature on the signature page, this subscription shall be irrevocable and the Investor shall be bound." The General Partner may accept or reject in its sole discretion. Applying the decision rules:

1. When did the investor sign and deliver? Meridian signed September 14, 2026 (Monday) (06a, p. 14); Covewood September 15; Sorrel Point September 16; Hallam and the Whitcombe Family Trust September 17.
2. Could the investor revoke after delivery? Yes, until the General Partner's acceptance (s. 2). The subscription is revocable until accepted, so delivery is not the sale.
3. Does the commitment depend on a later act of the issuer? Yes: the General Partner's countersignature. The investor is bound on that date. There is no minimum-raise condition or conditions-precedent list in the subscription or the LPA (LPA s. 3.3, p. 13, admits the investor on the GP's acceptance as evidenced by countersignature).

The earliest countersignature is **September 16, 2026 (Wednesday)**, Dana Whitcombe accepting the Meridian subscription (06a, p. 14). The other four were accepted September 18, 2026 (Friday). The closing memo (09, p. 1) and the register's Notes sheet (08) say the date of first sale is the Initial Closing Date, September 18, 2026; that is the closing label, not the contractual event, and the executed document controls (Section 2, item 2). Capital will first be called in October 2026 (09, p. 1); the call date is irrelevant to the test and a mandatory capital call does not start a new clock.
