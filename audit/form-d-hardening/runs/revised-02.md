### Date of first sale: contract reasoning

The Form D instruction defines the date of first sale as "the date on which the first investor is irrevocably contractually committed to invest, which, depending on the terms and conditions of the contract, could be the date on which the issuer receives the investor's subscription agreement or check" (Form D General Instructions; Securities Act Rules C&DI 257.02).

Applying the three questions in the skill to the earliest executed subscription:

1. **Signature and delivery.** Meridian Teachers' Retirement System signed its subscription on September 14, 2026 (Monday) (06a, p. 14). It is the earliest-signed subscription in the matter.
2. **Revocability.** Section 2 of the subscription agreement (05 form, p. 2, incorporated in each executed copy): "The Investor may withdraw this subscription by written notice at any time prior to the General Partner's acceptance. Upon acceptance by the General Partner, evidenced by its countersignature on the signature page, this subscription shall be irrevocable and the Investor shall be bound". The investor could withdraw until the GP countersigned.
3. **Dependence on a later act of the issuer.** The commitment depends on GP acceptance, evidenced by countersignature (s. 2; LPA s. 3.3, p. 13: admission "upon the General Partner's acceptance ... as evidenced by the General Partner's countersignature"). The GP "may accept or reject this subscription in whole or in part in its sole discretion" (s. 2).

The contract is in the "revocable until the issuer accepts" category. The date of first sale is therefore the earliest acceptance date: **September 16, 2026 (Wednesday)**, when Dana Whitcombe countersigned the Meridian subscription (06a, p. 14). The remaining four subscriptions were accepted September 18, 2026 (Friday) (06b, 06c, 06d, 06e, p. 14 each).

Not used: the Initial Closing date of September 18, 2026 adopted by the closing memo (09, p. 1) and the ledger Notes sheet (08 v4, Notes) ("first sale date = initial closing date 9/18/2026"). Both are rank 3 sources and both postdate the Meridian acceptance by two days; the executed document controls. Also not used: the first capital call (October 2026 per 09, p. 1), because payment is never the test. The Sorrel Point amendment accepted September 21, 2026 (06d-1, p. 2) is a change to an existing accepted commitment under the same offering and does not start a new clock.

Other candidate date, recorded for completeness: September 14, 2026 (Monday), the Meridian signature and delivery date. It would apply only if the subscription were irrevocable on delivery; section 2 says the opposite, so this is not treated as ambiguous. Its raw day 15 would be September 29, 2026 (Tuesday). Filing on or before September 29 moots the point (Section 4, judgment call 1).

### Amendment triggers going forward (no amendment is required now)

- **Annual.** If the offering is continuing, a Form D/A is due on or before the first anniversary of the filing of the notice (Rule 503(a)(3)(iii)). If the notice is filed October 1, 2026, the annual date is October 1, 2027 (Friday). Each filed amendment resets it.
- **Change-driven, likely within the offering period.** Subsequent closings may run until September 18, 2027 (LPA s. 3.5, p. 13; PPM p. 7). Changes in amount sold, amount remaining and total number of investors are excepted (Rule 503(a)(3)(ii)(F), (H)). Not excepted: a new related person; a new sales compensation recipient (for example Luis Ortiz if later determined to be a recipient, or a second placement agent); an increase in the total offering amount above $250,000,000 exceeding 10 percent cumulatively (a cap increase to more than $275,000,000 would require consent of a Majority in Interest under LPA s. 3.4 and an amendment); a decrease in the Item 11 minimum of more than 10 percent cumulatively; an increase in Item 15 commissions of more than 10 percent cumulatively measured against the figure filed. Each such change is due "as soon as practicable" after the change, not at the annual date.
- **Error-driven.** If a second Sorrel Point amendment to $15,000,000 surfaces after filing (Section 2, issue 2), the amount sold changes by an excepted item only (F), and the Item 15 increase from $540,000 to $600,000 is 11.1 percent, which exceeds the 10 percent cumulative threshold under exception (I) and would require a Form D/A as soon as practicable. Resolve the figure before filing to avoid this.
