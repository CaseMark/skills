**Date of first sale: the definition applied.** The Form D instructions define the date of first sale as the date on which the first investor is irrevocably contractually committed to invest, which, depending on the terms and conditions of the contract, could be the date on which the issuer receives the investor's subscription agreement or check. Subscription Agreement s. 2 (05, p. 2; executed on the same form in 06a-06k) provides that the investor "may withdraw this subscription by written notice at any time prior to the General Partner's acceptance" and that "upon acceptance by the General Partner, evidenced by its countersignature on the signature page, this subscription shall be irrevocable." Main LPA s. 3.3 (p. 13) confirms that admission occurs on countersignature. The investor is therefore revocable until the countersignature, and the date of first sale for each vehicle is the earliest countersignature date, not the investor's signature date, the "Initial Closing" label, the closing memo date or the register's closing date. Capital is first called in October 2026 (closing memo p. 1); payment is not the test.

## 4. Judgment calls

**J-1. One filing or three.** Question: whether the three issuers file separate Form Ds, one co-issuer Form D, or a Main/Feeder co-issuer filing with the Parallel Fund separate. Facts: one PPM and one Supplement offer all three vehicles; one general partner, one investment manager, one plan of financing; a shared $250,000,000 cap; the same exemption and exclusion (Rule 506(b); Section 3(c)(7)); overlapping sale dates (September 16-21, 2026); the Feeder is also an investor in the Main Fund (the staff's master-feeder example for a single filing). Options and consequences: (A) three separate new notices (recommended): Main Fund due October 1, Parallel Fund and Feeder due October 5; each issuer reports its own Items 13, 14 and 15 with no mixed counts; the pending-CIK risk is confined to the Main Fund for October 1; states that require one notice per issuer are served; the cost is three filings and three state notice sets. (B) one co-issuer filing, Main Fund primary: one date of first sale (September 16) and one deadline (October 1) for all three; all three CIKs and CCCs needed by October 1; one Item 13 (amount sold for the offering as a whole, $156,000,000 after removing the Feeder duplicate, or $171,000,000 if the Feeder's sales are counted as its own) and one Item 14 (which must either count the Feeder once or its three investors, with no clarification field to explain); fewer filings. (C) Main Fund and Feeder together (the staff's example), Parallel Fund separate: Main/Feeder due October 1 with two CIKs needed; Parallel Fund due October 5. Reason for the recommendation: the deadline risk and the cleaner, per-issuer presentation of Items 13 and 14 outweigh the convenience of one filing, and the Form D instructions and staff guidance permit either approach. Whether the vehicles' sales are one offering or separate offerings for integration purposes is a facts-and-circumstances question `[VERIFY]`; the memo errs toward the earlier date by recommending that all three be filed by October 1 if CIKs allow.
