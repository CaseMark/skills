### Date of first sale: contract reasoning

The Form D instruction defines the date of first sale as "the date on which the first investor is irrevocably contractually committed to invest, which, depending on the terms and conditions of the contract, could be the date on which the issuer receives the investor's subscription agreement or check."

Each executed subscription agreement uses the same form. Section 2 (Acceptance; Revocability), p. 2, provides: "The Investor may withdraw this subscription by written notice at any time prior to the General Partner's acceptance. Upon acceptance by the General Partner, evidenced by its countersignature on the signature page, this subscription shall be irrevocable and the Investor shall be bound to make the Capital Commitment set forth on the signature page." LPA s. 3.3, p. 13, is consistent: admission occurs on the General Partner's acceptance, evidenced by countersignature.

The three questions:

1. When did the investor sign and deliver? Meridian signed September 14, 2026 (Monday) (06a, p. 14), the earliest investor signature.
2. Could the investor revoke after delivery? Yes, until the General Partner's acceptance (s. 2, p. 2).
3. Does the commitment depend on a later act of the issuer? Yes: countersignature by the General Partner. Meridian was countersigned September 16, 2026 (Wednesday) by Dana Whitcombe, Managing Member (06a, p. 14). This is the earliest acceptance in the matter; the other four subscriptions were accepted September 18, 2026 (Friday) (06b, 06c, 06d, 06e, p. 14 each).

The contract is not silent or ambiguous: the subscription is revocable until acceptance, so the date of first sale is the acceptance date, September 16, 2026. The investor signature date (September 14, 2026) is not the date of irrevocable commitment, and the Initial Closing Date (September 18, 2026) used by the closing memo (09, p. 1) and the Cobalt register Notes sheet (08, sheet "Notes") is two days after the first acceptance and is not the governing event. Capital is first called in October 2026 (09, p. 1) and is never the test.

```
Date of first sale: September 16, 2026 (Wednesday)
Raw day 15:         October 1, 2026 (Thursday)
Due date:           October 1, 2026 (Thursday)   [not rolled]
```

Rejected candidate for the record: if the closing memo's September 18, 2026 (Friday) were used, raw day 15 would be October 3, 2026 (Saturday), rolling to October 5, 2026 (Monday). Filing by October 1, 2026 satisfies both readings; do not rely on the later date.

"Holiday" is not defined in Rule 503; federal legal holidays are assumed `[VERIFY]`. No federal holiday falls between September 16 and October 1, 2026 (Columbus Day is October 12, 2026). File before 5:30 p.m. Eastern on the due date: a submission after that time is deemed filed the next business day, and Form D is not among the submission types dated same-day after 5:30 p.m. `[VERIFY]`.

**2. Item 11 minimum investment (four questions).**

1. Stated minimum: $5,000,000 in both the LPA (s. 3.2, p. 12) and the PPM (p. 7); they agree.
