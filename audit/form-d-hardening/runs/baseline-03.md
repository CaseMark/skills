**Date of first sale: the form's definition.** The Form D instructions define the date of first sale as the date on which the first investor is irrevocably contractually committed to invest, which, depending on the terms and conditions of the contract, could be the date on which the issuer receives the investor's subscription agreement or check (Form D General Instructions p. 7; Item 7 instruction p. 10; Securities Act Rules C&DI 257.02).

**Contract reasoning (all three vehicles use the same subscription form, document 05).** Section 2 (p. 2): "The Investor may withdraw this subscription by written notice at any time prior to the General Partner's acceptance. Upon acceptance by the General Partner, evidenced by its countersignature on the signature page, this subscription shall be irrevocable." LPA s. 3.3 (Main p. 13) likewise makes admission turn on countersignature. The investor is therefore not irrevocably committed on signing or delivery; it is committed on the date of the GP's countersignature. The scheduled closing date, the register's "Closing date" column, the closing memo's statement that "the date of first sale is the Initial Closing Date, September 18, 2026" (document 09 p. 1), and the register's note "Form D first sale date for all vehicles: 9/18/2026" (document 08, sheet Program) are not the test and are wrong for the Main Fund. Capital is first called in October 2026 (closing memo p. 1); payment is never the test.
