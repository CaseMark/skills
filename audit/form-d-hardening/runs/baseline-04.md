## Structure classification

Two issuers, each a Delaware limited partnership formed within the last five years (Main Fund: Feb. 10, 2026; Parallel Fund: Aug. 3, 2026), each a pooled investment fund (private equity, growth-equity strategy), each with the same general partner (Larkspur GP III, LLC) and the same investment manager (Larkspur Capital Management LLC, "LCM"). The Parallel Fund invests side by side with the Main Fund on the same terms under one PPM plus Supplement No. 1, with one $250,000,000 hard cap shared by both vehicles. Both offerings rely on Rule 506(b) and the Investment Company Act Section 3(c)(7) exclusion (LPA s. 12.1; PPM p. 8; Supplement s. 12.1 equivalent). Both are new offerings: no Form D has been filed for either issuer, neither has a CIK, and Form ID applications for both were submitted on September 22, 2026 and are pending. Each vehicle sells its own interests under its own subscription agreements, so each is a separate issuer; whether to file one co-issuer Form D or two separate Form Ds is a judgment call (Section 4, J-2). This memo drafts two separate notices (the recommended approach) and shows the consequence of the co-issuer alternative.

### Date of first sale: the test applied

The Form D instructions define the date of first sale as "the date on which the first investor is irrevocably contractually committed to invest, which, depending on the terms and conditions of the contract, could be the date on which the issuer receives the investor's subscription agreement or check" (Form D General Instructions p. 7; Item 7 instruction p. 10; Securities Act Rules C&DI 257.02).

The subscription contract answers the three questions for every investor in both vehicles. Subscription Agreement s. 2 (Acceptance; Revocability), p. 2: "The Investor may withdraw this subscription by written notice at any time prior to the General Partner's acceptance. Upon acceptance by the General Partner, evidenced by its countersignature on the signature page, this subscription shall be irrevocable and the Investor shall be bound to make the Capital Commitment." LPA s. 3.3 (p. 13) is to the same effect: admission is upon the GP's acceptance "as evidenced by the General Partner's countersignature." The subscription is therefore **revocable until the issuer accepts**, and the date of first sale is the earliest GP countersignature date for each vehicle, not the investor's signature date and not the closing date. No condition precedent, minimum raise or escrow applies.

### Amendments (Rule 503(a)(3)), for the record

No amendment is due now. Once each notice is filed: the annual amendment is due on or before the first anniversary of that filing while the offering continues (subsequent closings may run up to twelve months after the initial closing, LPA s. 3.5, p. 13; PPM p. 7), and a change-driven amendment, if one is filed first, resets the anniversary to its own filing date. Foreseeable change triggers: (i) a new Item 12 recipient or a new Item 3 related person (never excepted); (ii) an increase in Item 15 commissions of more than 10 percent cumulatively against the filed figure, which a 2 percent fee on any later Covered Investor (Placement Agreement Exhibit B lists Pell & Ostrander Partners LP) would exceed against a filed $250,000; (iii) a cap increase above $250,000,000 of more than 10 percent cumulatively; (iv) a decrease in the Item 11 minimum of more than 10 percent. Increases in amount sold and in investor counts (exceptions (F) and (H)) do not by themselves require an amendment.
