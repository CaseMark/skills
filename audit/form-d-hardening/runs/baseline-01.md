**Date of first sale: the rule and the contract reasoning.** The Form D instructions define the date of first sale as "the date on which the first investor is irrevocably contractually committed to invest, which, depending on the terms and conditions of the contract, could be the date on which the issuer receives the investor's subscription agreement or check." The subscriptions here are not irrevocable on delivery. Subscription Agreement s. 2 (Acceptance; Revocability, p. 2, identical in the form and in each executed agreement) provides that the Investor "may withdraw this subscription by written notice at any time prior to the General Partner's acceptance," that "[u]pon acceptance by the General Partner, evidenced by its countersignature on the signature page, this subscription shall be irrevocable and the Investor shall be bound," and that the General Partner may accept or reject in its sole discretion. LPA s. 3.3 (p. 13) likewise makes admission turn on the General Partner's countersignature. The three questions from the skill:

1. *When did the investor sign and deliver?* Meridian signed September 14, 2026 (Monday) (06a p. 14), the earliest investor signature in the set.
2. *Could the investor revoke after delivery, and until when?* Yes, by written notice at any time before the General Partner's acceptance (s. 2).
3. *Does the investor's commitment depend on a later act of the issuer?* Yes: acceptance evidenced by countersignature (s. 2; LPA s. 3.3).

The investor therefore became irrevocably bound on the acceptance date. The earliest countersignature in the set is Meridian's, dated **September 16, 2026 (Wednesday)**, by Dana Whitcombe, Managing Member (06a p. 14). The other four subscriptions were countersigned September 18, 2026 (Friday) (06b-06e, p. 14 each). No condition precedent to the investor's obligation (minimum raise, escrow, conditions list) appears in the subscription agreement or the LPA; capital is called later on ten business days' notice (LPA s. 3.1, p. 12) and the first call is planned for October 2026 (Closing Memo p. 1), which is irrelevant to the test because payment is never the test for a fund.

**Conflict.** The Closing Memo (p. 1) states "[f]or Form D purposes the date of first sale is the Initial Closing Date, September 18, 2026," and the Register sheet "Notes" states "first sale date = initial closing date 9/18/2026." Both are rank-3 sources; the executed and countersigned subscription agreement (rank 1) controls, and the closing date is not a substitute for the date of irrevocable commitment. The value used is September 16, 2026. The client should be told that its working date is wrong and that the deadline is four calendar days earlier than it may be planning for (Section 2, issue 2). The alternative candidate date (September 18, 2026) and its deadline are recorded in Section 4, judgment call 1; filing by October 1 satisfies both.

- *Excepted changes.* Changes solely in the amount sold, the amount remaining, the total number of investors, the Item 12 states of solicitation, or a related person's address do not require an amendment, but every amendment filed for any reason must give current information in every item.

The form's text names executive officers, directors, persons performing similar functions (general partners, managing partners, managing members) and promoters; it does not mention investment advisers or managers. Listing the entity general partner alongside its principals is observed practice, not form text `[VERIFY]`; listing adviser officers is firm practice `[VERIFY]`. **Recommended: (a), with the entity general partner and both Managing Members, applying the practice of listing the entity plus its principals and not the practice of adding adviser officers**, because the LPA vests full, exclusive and complete authority in the General Partner (s. 5.1) and the Managing Members perform the executive officer and director functions (GP LLC Agreement s. 4.1).
